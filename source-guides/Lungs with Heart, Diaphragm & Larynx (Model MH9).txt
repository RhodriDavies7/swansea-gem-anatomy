Major organs and regions (A–G)
A
    Identification→Larynx #Identification #Organ #MajorOrgans #MH9
    Function→Maintains the airway, protects the lower respiratory tract during swallowing and produces phonation. #Function #Organ #MajorOrgans #MH9
    Location→Anterior neck between pharynx and trachea, approximately C3–C6. #Location #Organ #MajorOrgans #MH9
    
B
    Identification→Thyroid gland #Identification #Gland #MajorOrgans #MH9
    Location→Anterior neck, with lobes on either side of the upper trachea connected by an isthmus. #Location #Gland #MajorOrgans #MH9
    Function→Produces thyroid hormones and calcitonin. #Function #Gland #MajorOrgans #MH9
    Blood supply→Mainly superior and inferior thyroid arteries. #BloodSupply #Gland #MajorOrgans #MH9
    
C
    Identification→Trachea #Identification #Tube #MajorOrgans #MH9
    Function→Conducts air between larynx and main bronchi. #Function #Tube #MajorOrgans #MH9
    Structure→Supported by C-shaped hyaline cartilage rings with a posterior membranous wall. #Structure #Tube #MajorOrgans #MH9
    Course→Begins at C6 and bifurcates at the carina around the sternal angle/T4–T5 level. #Course #Tube #MajorOrgans #MH9
    
D
    Identification→Oesophagus #Identification #Tube #MajorOrgans #MH9
    Function→Transports food and fluid from pharynx to stomach by peristalsis. #Function #Tube #MajorOrgans #MH9
    Course→Begins at C6, descends through posterior mediastinum and passes through diaphragm at T10. #Course #Tube #MajorOrgans #MH9
    Relations→Lies posterior to trachea in the neck and upper thorax. #Relations #Tube #MajorOrgans #MH9
    
E
    Identification→Lung #Identification #Organ #MajorOrgans #MH9
    Function→Gas exchange between alveolar air and pulmonary capillary blood. #Function #Organ #MajorOrgans #MH9
    Structure→Right lung has three lobes; left lung normally has two lobes and a cardiac notch. #Structure #Organ #MajorOrgans #MH9
    
F
    Identification→Heart #Identification #Organ #MajorOrgans #MH9
    Function→Pumps blood through pulmonary and systemic circulations. #Function #Organ #MajorOrgans #MH9
    Location→Middle mediastinum within the pericardium. #Location #Organ #MajorOrgans #MH9
    Parts→Four chambers: right and left atria, right and left ventricles. #Parts #Organ #MajorOrgans #MH9
    
G
    Identification→Diaphragm #Identification #Muscle #MajorOrgans #MH9
    Actions→Primary muscle of inspiration; contraction increases thoracic volume. #Action #Muscle #MajorOrgans #MH9
    Innervation→Phrenic nerves (C3–C5) provide motor supply. #Innervation #Muscle #MajorOrgans #MH9
    Origin→Xiphoid process, lower six ribs/costal cartilages and upper lumbar vertebrae via crura. #Origin #Muscle #MajorOrgans #MH9
    Insertion→Central tendon. #Insertion #Muscle #MajorOrgans #MH9

3

4

6

7

    Branches→Divides into left upper and lower lobar bronchi. #Branches #Bronchus #LarynxAndAirway #MH9

9
