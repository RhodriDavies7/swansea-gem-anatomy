1
    Identification→Scapula #Identification #Bone #NS17
    Articulations→Articulates with the humeral head at the glenohumeral joint and with the clavicle at the acromioclavicular joint. #Articulations #Bone #NS17
    Landmarks→Spine, acromion, coracoid process, glenoid cavity, supraglenoid and infraglenoid tubercles, supraspinous/infraspinous fossae, subscapular fossa, borders and angles. #Landmarks #Bone #NS17
    Muscle attachments→Rotator cuff muscles, deltoid, trapezius, biceps/triceps long heads, coracobrachialis, pectoralis minor, rhomboids, levator scapulae and teres muscles attach to the scapula. #Attachments #Bone #NS17
    Orientation→Glenoid faces laterally; spine is posterior; subscapular fossa is anterior; inferior angle points down. #Orientation #Bone #NS17
    
2
    Identification→Acromion (of scapula) #Identification #BonyLandmark #NS17
    Bone→Scapula. #Bone #BonyLandmark #NS17
    Attachments→Deltoid arises from its lateral border and trapezius inserts on its medial/superior aspect; acromioclavicular and coraco-acromial ligaments attach nearby. #Attachments #BonyLandmark #NS17
    Relations→Forms the summit of the shoulder and the roof over the glenohumeral joint/subacromial space. #Relations #BonyLandmark #NS17
    Clinical relevance→Subacromial impingement may involve structures passing beneath the acromion. #Clinical #BonyLandmark #NS17
    
3
    Identification→Spine of scapula #Identification #BonyLandmark #NS17
    Bone→Scapula. #Bone #BonyLandmark #NS17
    Attachments→Trapezius attaches to the superior lip and deltoid to the inferior lip. #Attachments #BonyLandmark #NS17
    Relations→Divides supraspinous and infraspinous fossae and expands laterally into the acromion. #Relations #BonyLandmark #NS17
    Clinical relevance→Palpable posterior landmark useful for orienting the scapula. #Clinical #BonyLandmark #NS17
    
4
    Identification→Coracoid process (of scapula) #Identification #BonyLandmark #NS17
    Bone→Scapula. #Bone #BonyLandmark #NS17
    Attachments→Short head of biceps and coracobrachialis arise from its tip; pectoralis minor inserts medially; coracoacromial, coracoclavicular and coracohumeral ligaments attach to it. #Attachments #BonyLandmark #NS17
    Relations→Projects anterolaterally above the glenoid and is palpable below the lateral clavicle. #Relations #BonyLandmark #NS17
    Clinical relevance→Important landmark in shoulder surgery and in stabilisation of the acromioclavicular complex. #Clinical #BonyLandmark #NS17
    
5
    Identification→Clavicle #Identification #Bone #NS17
    Articulations→Articulates medially with the manubrium and laterally with the acromion. #Articulations #Bone #NS17
    Landmarks→Sternal end, acromial end, conoid tubercle, trapezoid line, subclavian groove and impression for costoclavicular ligament. #Landmarks #Bone #NS17
    Muscle attachments→Pectoralis major, deltoid, trapezius, sternocleidomastoid and subclavius attach to the clavicle. #Attachments #Bone #NS17
    Orientation→Sternal end is bulky; acromial end is flattened; conoid tubercle lies inferoposteriorly near the lateral end. #Orientation #Bone #NS17
    
6
    Identification→Humerus #Identification #Bone #NS17
    Articulations→Articulates with the scapula proximally and with the radius and ulna distally. #Articulations #Bone #NS17
    Landmarks→Head, anatomical/surgical necks, greater/lesser tubercles, intertubercular sulcus, deltoid tuberosity, radial groove, condyles and epicondyles. #Landmarks #Bone #NS17
    Muscle attachments→Rotator cuff inserts around the tubercles; pectoralis major, latissimus dorsi and teres major attach around the intertubercular sulcus. #Attachments #Bone #NS17
    Orientation→Head faces medially, superiorly and slightly posteriorly; greater tubercle is lateral. #Orientation #Bone #NS17
    
7
    Identification→Glenohumeral ligament (thickening of joint capsule) #Identification #Ligament #NS17
    Attachments→Capsular thickenings extending from the glenoid/labrum region to the anatomical neck of the humerus. #Attachments #Ligament #NS17
    Parts→Superior, middle and inferior glenohumeral ligaments. #Parts #Ligament #NS17
    Function→Reinforces the anterior glenohumeral capsule and limits excessive translation depending on arm position. #Function #Ligament #NS17
    Clinical relevance→The inferior glenohumeral ligament complex is particularly important in resisting anterior dislocation. #Clinical #Ligament #NS17
    
8
    Identification→Coracohumeral ligament #Identification #Ligament #NS17
    Attachments→Extends from the lateral base of the coracoid process to the greater and lesser tubercle region of the humerus, blending with the capsule. #Attachments #Ligament #NS17
    Function→Reinforces the superior glenohumeral capsule and helps resist inferior translation of the humeral head. #Function #Ligament #NS17
    Relations→Lies superior to the glenohumeral joint and blends with rotator cuff/capsular fibres. #Relations #Ligament #NS17
    
9
    Identification→Coraco-acromial ligament #Identification #Ligament #NS17
    Attachments→Extends from the coracoid process to the acromion. #Attachments #Ligament #NS17
    Function→Forms the coracoacromial arch, limiting superior displacement of the humeral head. #Function #Ligament #NS17
    Relations→Forms the superior roof over the humeral head, subacromial bursa and supraspinatus tendon. #Relations #Ligament #NS17
    Clinical relevance→Can contribute to subacromial impingement. #Clinical #Ligament #NS17
    
10
    Identification→Conoid part of coracoclavicular ligament #Identification #Ligament #NS17
    Attachments→Extends from the base of the coracoid process to the conoid tubercle on the inferior clavicle. #Attachments #Ligament #NS17
    Function→Resists superior displacement of the clavicle and provides major vertical stability to the acromioclavicular joint. #Function #Ligament #NS17
    Relations→More medial and more vertically oriented than the trapezoid part. #Relations #Ligament #NS17
    Clinical relevance→Coracoclavicular ligament disruption is important in higher-grade acromioclavicular separations. #Clinical #Ligament #NS17
    
11
    Identification→Trapezoid part of coracoclavicular ligament #Identification #Ligament #NS17
    Attachments→Extends from the superior surface of the coracoid process to the trapezoid line on the inferior clavicle. #Attachments #Ligament #NS17
    Function→Stabilises the acromioclavicular joint and resists medial displacement/compression of the scapula relative to the clavicle. #Function #Ligament #NS17
    Relations→More lateral and more oblique/horizontal than the conoid part. #Relations #Ligament #NS17
    
12
    Identification→Subscapularis muscle #Identification #Muscle #NS17
    Actions→Medially rotates and adducts the arm and stabilises the humeral head in the glenoid. #Action #Muscle #NS17
    Innervation→Upper and lower subscapular nerves (C5–C7). #Innervation #Muscle #NS17
    Origin→Subscapular fossa of scapula. #Origin #Muscle #NS17
    Insertion→Lesser tubercle of humerus and anterior shoulder joint capsule. #Insertion #Muscle #NS17
    
13
    Identification→Long head of biceps brachii #Identification #Muscle #NS17
    Actions→Supinates the forearm and flexes the elbow; assists shoulder flexion and helps stabilise the humeral head. #Action #Muscle #NS17
    Innervation→Musculocutaneous nerve (C5–C6). #Innervation #Muscle #NS17
    Origin→Supraglenoid tubercle of scapula and adjacent superior glenoid labrum. #Origin #Muscle #NS17
    Course→Tendon passes intra-articularly but extrasynovially across the humeral head and then through the intertubercular sulcus. #Course #Muscle #NS17
    Insertion→Radial tuberosity and forearm fascia via the bicipital aponeurosis. #Insertion #Muscle #NS17
    
14
    Identification→Supraspinatus muscle #Identification #Muscle #NS17
    Actions→Initiates the first ~15° of arm abduction and stabilises the humeral head in the glenoid. #Action #Muscle #NS17
    Innervation→Suprascapular nerve (C5–C6). #Innervation #Muscle #NS17
    Origin→Supraspinous fossa of scapula and overlying fascia. #Origin #Muscle #NS17
    Insertion→Superior facet of greater tubercle of humerus and shoulder joint capsule. #Insertion #Muscle #NS17
    
15
    Identification→Infraspinatus muscle #Identification #Muscle #NS17
    Actions→Laterally rotates the arm and stabilises the humeral head in the glenoid. #Action #Muscle #NS17
    Innervation→Suprascapular nerve (C5–C6). #Innervation #Muscle #NS17
    Origin→Infraspinous fossa of scapula and overlying fascia. #Origin #Muscle #NS17
    Insertion→Middle facet of greater tubercle of humerus and shoulder joint capsule. #Insertion #Muscle #NS17
    
16
    Identification→Subacromial bursa #Identification #Bursa #NS17
    Location→Between the supraspinatus tendon/deltoid and the acromion-coracoacromial arch. #Location #Bursa #NS17
    Function→Reduces friction during movement of the rotator cuff and deltoid beneath the coracoacromial arch. #Function #Bursa #NS17
    Relations→Closely related to the supraspinatus tendon and subdeltoid region; usually does not communicate with the glenohumeral joint. #Relations #Bursa #NS17
    Clinical relevance→Inflammation causes painful arc symptoms and may accompany rotator cuff impingement. #Clinical #Bursa #NS17
    
17
    Identification→Subcoracoid bursa #Identification #Bursa #NS17
    Location→Lies beneath the coracoid process, between it and adjacent subscapularis/capsular structures. #Location #Bursa #NS17
    Function→Reduces friction between structures moving beneath the coracoid process. #Function #Bursa #NS17
    Relations→Related to the subscapularis tendon and anterior shoulder capsule; may communicate variably with nearby bursae or joint recesses. #Relations #Bursa #NS17
    Clinical relevance→Can become inflamed and contribute to anterior shoulder pain. #Clinical #Bursa #NS17
    
18
    Identification→Bursa between heads of teres major and latissimus dorsi #Identification #Bursa #NS17
    Location→Lies between the adjacent tendinous/muscular portions of teres major and latissimus dorsi near their humeral insertions. #Location #Bursa #NS17
    Function→Reduces friction between teres major and latissimus dorsi during shoulder movement. #Function #Bursa #NS17
    Relations→Located near the medial and floor regions of the intertubercular sulcus where teres major and latissimus dorsi insert. #Relations #Bursa #NS17
