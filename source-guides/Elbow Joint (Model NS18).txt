a
    Identification→Humerus #Identification #Bone #NS18
    Articulations→Articulates distally with the ulna at the trochlea and with the radius at the capitulum. #Articulations #Bone #NS18
    Landmarks→Capitulum, trochlea, medial and lateral epicondyles, coronoid fossa, radial fossa and olecranon fossa. #Landmarks #Bone #NS18
    Muscle attachments→Common flexor origin attaches to the medial epicondyle; common extensor origin attaches to the lateral epicondyle; brachialis and triceps attach proximally/distally around the humerus. #Attachments #Bone #NS18
    Orientation→Capitulum lies lateral and trochlea medial; olecranon fossa is posterior. #Orientation #Bone #NS18
    
b
    Identification→Ulna #Identification #Bone #NS18
    Articulations→Articulates with the humeral trochlea at the trochlear notch and with the radius at the proximal and distal radioulnar joints. #Articulations #Bone #NS18
    Landmarks→Olecranon, coronoid process, trochlear notch, radial notch and ulnar tuberosity. #Landmarks #Bone #NS18
    Muscle attachments→Triceps inserts on olecranon; brachialis inserts on coronoid process/ulnar tuberosity; pronator teres and flexor muscles attach in the proximal forearm region. #Attachments #Bone #NS18
    Orientation→Olecranon projects proximally/posteriorly; radial notch lies laterally. #Orientation #Bone #NS18
    
c
    Identification→Radius #Identification #Bone #NS18
    Articulations→Articulates proximally with capitulum of humerus and radial notch of ulna; distally with scaphoid/lunate and ulna. #Articulations #Bone #NS18
    Landmarks→Head, neck, radial tuberosity and interosseous border. #Landmarks #Bone #NS18
    Muscle attachments→Biceps brachii inserts on radial tuberosity; supinator wraps around proximal radius; pronator teres inserts on lateral radius. #Attachments #Bone #NS18
    Orientation→Radial head is proximal and disc-shaped; radial tuberosity lies medially. #Orientation #Bone #NS18
    
1
    Identification→Articular capsule #Identification #JointCapsule #NS18
    Attachments→Proximally attaches around the distal humeral articular margins; distally attaches to the ulna and blends with the annular ligament around the radius. #Attachments #JointCapsule #NS18
    Function→Encloses and stabilises the elbow joint while allowing flexion-extension and forearm rotation at the proximal radioulnar joint. #Function #JointCapsule #NS18
    Relations→Reinforced medially and laterally by collateral ligaments; relatively lax anteriorly and posteriorly to permit movement. #Relations #JointCapsule #NS18
    
2
    Identification→Radial (lateral) collateral ligament #Identification #Ligament #NS18
    Attachments→Extends from the lateral epicondyle of humerus and blends mainly with the annular ligament. #Attachments #Ligament #NS18
    Function→Provides lateral stability to the elbow and resists varus stress. #Function #Ligament #NS18
    Relations→Part of the lateral collateral ligament complex, adjacent to the annular ligament of the radius. #Relations #Ligament #NS18
    Clinical relevance→Injury to the lateral collateral complex can contribute to posterolateral rotatory instability. #Clinical #Ligament #NS18
    
3
    Identification→Ulnar (medial) collateral ligament #Identification #Ligament #NS18
    Attachments→Extends from the medial epicondyle of humerus to the coronoid process and olecranon of ulna. #Attachments #Ligament #NS18
    Parts→Anterior, posterior and transverse bands. #Parts #Ligament #NS18
    Function→Provides medial stability and resists valgus stress at the elbow. #Function #Ligament #NS18
    Clinical relevance→Can be injured by repetitive valgus loading, especially in throwing athletes. #Clinical #Ligament #NS18
    
4
    Identification→Annular ligament of the radius #Identification #Ligament #NS18
    Attachments→Attaches to the anterior and posterior margins of the radial notch of the ulna and encircles the radial head. #Attachments #Ligament #NS18
    Function→Holds the radial head against the ulna while permitting pronation and supination. #Function #Ligament #NS18
    Relations→Forms a fibro-osseous ring with the radial notch around the radial head. #Relations #Ligament #NS18
    Clinical relevance→In young children, radial head subluxation from the annular ligament causes nursemaid's elbow. #Clinical #Ligament #NS18
    
5
    Identification→Tendon of biceps brachii #Identification #Tendon #NS18
    Origin→Continuation of the biceps brachii muscle distally. #Origin #Tendon #NS18
    Insertion→Inserts on the radial tuberosity; the bicipital aponeurosis blends with forearm fascia. #Insertion #Tendon #NS18
    Function→Transmits biceps force for forearm supination and elbow flexion. #Function #Tendon #NS18
    Relations→Passes anterior to the elbow joint into the cubital fossa before inserting on the radius. #Relations #Tendon #NS18
    Clinical relevance→Distal biceps rupture weakens supination more than elbow flexion. #Clinical #Tendon #NS18
    
6
    Identification→Oblique cord #Identification #Ligament #NS18
    Attachments→Extends from the ulna just distal to the radial notch to the radius just distal to the radial tuberosity. #Attachments #Ligament #NS18
    Function→Helps bind the proximal radius and ulna and may limit excessive distal displacement of the radius. #Function #Ligament #NS18
    Relations→Runs inferolaterally from ulna to radius, opposite in fibre direction to much of the interosseous membrane. #Relations #Ligament #NS18
    
7
    Identification→Interosseous membrane #Identification #FibrousJoint #NS18
    Attachments→Broad fibrous sheet spanning the interosseous borders of radius and ulna. #Attachments #FibrousJoint #NS18
    Function→Binds radius and ulna, transmits load from radius to ulna and provides muscle attachment. #Function #FibrousJoint #NS18
    Fibre direction→Most fibres run inferomedially from radius to ulna. #Orientation #FibrousJoint #NS18
    Relations→Separates anterior and posterior compartments of the forearm and contains openings for interosseous vessels. #Relations #FibrousJoint #NS18
