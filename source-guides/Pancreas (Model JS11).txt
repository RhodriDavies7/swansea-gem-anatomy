Major organs
A
    Identification→Pancreas #Identification #Organ #MajorOrgans #JS11
    Location→Retroperitoneal organ extending transversely from the duodenum toward the spleen. #Location #Organ #MajorOrgans #JS11
    Parts→Head, uncinate process, neck, body and tail. #Parts #Organ #MajorOrgans #JS11
    Function→Exocrine secretion of digestive enzymes and bicarbonate; endocrine secretion including insulin and glucagon. #Function #Organ #MajorOrgans #JS11
    Blood supply→Mainly pancreatic branches of splenic artery and superior/inferior pancreaticoduodenal arteries. #BloodSupply #Organ #MajorOrgans #JS11
    Relations→Head lies within the C-shaped duodenum; tail approaches the splenic hilum. #Relations #Organ #MajorOrgans #JS11
    
B
    Identification→Spleen #Identification #Organ #MajorOrgans #JS11
    Location→Left upper quadrant, deep to ribs 9–11. #Location #Organ #MajorOrgans #JS11
    Function→Filters blood, removes senescent erythrocytes and supports immune function. #Function #Organ #MajorOrgans #JS11
    Blood supply→Splenic artery; venous drainage via splenic vein. #BloodSupply #Organ #MajorOrgans #JS11
    Relations→Related to stomach, left kidney, colon and tail of pancreas. #Relations #Organ #MajorOrgans #JS11
    
C
    Identification→Duodenum #Identification #Organ #MajorOrgans #JS11
    Parts→Superior, descending, horizontal and ascending parts. #Parts #Organ #MajorOrgans #JS11
    Function→Receives gastric chyme, bile and pancreatic secretions and begins major small-intestinal digestion. #Function #Organ #MajorOrgans #JS11
    Relations→C-shaped around the pancreatic head. #Relations #Organ #MajorOrgans #JS11
    Blood supply→Superior pancreaticoduodenal branches from gastroduodenal artery and inferior pancreaticoduodenal branches from SMA. #BloodSupply #Organ #MajorOrgans #JS11
    
Pancreatic and biliary ducts
1
    Identification→Major pancreatic duct (duct of Wirsung) #Identification #Duct #Ducts #JS11
    Course→Runs from the tail through the body toward the pancreatic head. #Course #Duct #Ducts #JS11
    Termination→Usually joins the common bile duct to form the hepatopancreatic ampulla and opens at the major duodenal papilla. #Termination #Duct #Ducts #JS11
    Function→Drains most exocrine pancreatic secretions into the duodenum. #Function #Duct #Ducts #JS11
    Clinical relevance→Obstruction can contribute to pancreatitis. #Clinical #Duct #Ducts #JS11
    
2
    Identification→Accessory pancreatic duct (duct of Santorini) #Identification #Duct #Ducts #JS11
    Course→Drains part of the pancreatic head and may communicate with the major pancreatic duct. #Course #Duct #Ducts #JS11
    Termination→Typically opens into the minor duodenal papilla. #Termination #Duct #Ducts #JS11
    Function→Provides an additional drainage route for pancreatic secretions. #Function #Duct #Ducts #JS11
    
3
    Identification→Common bile duct #Identification #Duct #Ducts #JS11
    Formation→Formed by union of the common hepatic duct and cystic duct. #Formation #Duct #Ducts #JS11
    Course→Descends in the hepatoduodenal ligament, passes posterior to the first part of the duodenum and through/behind the pancreatic head. #Course #Duct #Ducts #JS11
    Termination→Usually joins the major pancreatic duct and opens at the major duodenal papilla. #Termination #Duct #Ducts #JS11
    Function→Carries bile to the second part of the duodenum. #Function #Duct #Ducts #JS11
    
4
    Identification→Longitudinal fold of the duodenum #Identification #MucosalFold #Ducts #JS11
    Location→Longitudinal mucosal fold on the medial wall of the descending duodenum. #Location #MucosalFold #Ducts #JS11
    Relations→The major duodenal papilla lies on the fold. #Relations #MucosalFold #Ducts #JS11
    Function→Marks the region where bile and pancreatic secretions enter the duodenum. #Function #MucosalFold #Ducts #JS11
    
Veins
5
    Identification→Portal vein #Identification #Vein #Veins #JS11
    Formation→Usually formed by union of the superior mesenteric and splenic veins behind the neck of the pancreas. #Formation #Vein #Veins #JS11
    Course→Ascends posterior to the first part of the duodenum and within the hepatoduodenal ligament to the liver. #Course #Vein #Veins #JS11
    Drainage→Carries nutrient-rich venous blood from the gastrointestinal tract, spleen, pancreas and gallbladder to the liver. #Drainage #Vein #Veins #JS11
    Clinical relevance→Portal hypertension can cause varices, ascites and portosystemic collateral formation. #Clinical #Vein #Veins #JS11
    
6
    Identification→Splenic vein #Identification #Vein #Veins #JS11
    Formation→Receives venous drainage from the spleen and tributaries from pancreas and stomach. #Formation #Vein #Veins #JS11
    Course→Runs along the posterior surface of the pancreas. #Course #Vein #Veins #JS11
    Termination→Joins the superior mesenteric vein to form the portal vein. #Termination #Vein #Veins #JS11
    Relations→Closely related to the posterior surface of the pancreatic body. #Relations #Vein #Veins #JS11
    
7
    Identification→Superior mesenteric vein #Identification #Vein #Veins #JS11
    Formation→Formed by tributaries draining the small intestine and much of the large intestine. #Formation #Vein #Veins #JS11
    Course→Ascends in the root of the mesentery and passes anterior to the third part of the duodenum. #Course #Vein #Veins #JS11
    Termination→Joins the splenic vein behind the neck of the pancreas to form the portal vein. #Termination #Vein #Veins #JS11
    Drainage→Drains the midgut-derived gastrointestinal tract. #Drainage #Vein #Veins #JS11
    
Arteries
8
    Identification→Coeliac trunk #Identification #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    Origin→Arises from the abdominal aorta around T12, just below the aortic hiatus. #Origin #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    Branches→Classically divides into left gastric, splenic and common hepatic arteries. #Branches #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    Supply→Supplies foregut-derived abdominal organs. #Supply #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    
9
    Identification→Hepatic artery #Identification #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    Origin→Common hepatic artery is a branch of the coeliac trunk; proper hepatic artery continues after giving the gastroduodenal artery. #Origin #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    Course→Proper hepatic artery ascends in the hepatoduodenal ligament. #Course #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    Branches→Commonly gives right and left hepatic branches distally. #Branches #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    Supply→Provides arterial blood to liver and parts of the biliary system. #Supply #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    
10
    Identification→Splenic artery #Identification #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    Origin→Branch of the coeliac trunk. #Origin #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    Course→Runs tortuously along the superior border of the pancreas toward the spleen. #Course #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    Branches→Gives pancreatic branches and short gastric/left gastro-omental branches. #Branches #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    Supply→Supplies spleen and contributes to blood supply of pancreas and stomach. #Supply #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    
11
    Identification→Gastric artery #Identification #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    Origin→The left gastric artery is a branch of the coeliac trunk. #Origin #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    Course→Runs toward the lesser curvature of the stomach. #Course #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    Supply→Supplies the stomach and lower oesophagus. #Supply #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    Model note→The supplied model key states 'Gastric artery'; this card uses the left gastric artery as the usual named coeliac branch. #ModelNote #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    
12
    Identification→Gastroduodenal artery #Identification #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    Origin→Usually arises from the common hepatic artery. #Origin #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    Course→Descends posterior to the first part of the duodenum. #Course #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    Branches→Gives superior pancreaticoduodenal and right gastro-omental arteries. #Branches #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    Supply→Supplies proximal duodenum, pancreatic head and part of stomach. #Supply #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    Clinical relevance→Posterior duodenal ulcers may erode this artery and cause major haemorrhage. #Clinical #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    
13
    Identification→Superior mesenteric artery #Identification #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    Origin→Arises from the anterior abdominal aorta at approximately L1. #Origin #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    Course→Passes posterior to the neck of the pancreas and anterior to the third part of the duodenum. #Course #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    Branches→Gives inferior pancreaticoduodenal, intestinal, ileocolic, right colic and middle colic branches. #Branches #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    Supply→Supplies the midgut. #Supply #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    
14
    Identification→Inferior pancreaticoduodenal artery #Identification #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    Origin→Usually the first branch of the superior mesenteric artery. #Origin #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    Course→Runs around the head of the pancreas and duodenum. #Course #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    Branches→Typically divides into anterior and posterior branches. #Branches #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    Supply→Supplies the inferior pancreatic head and distal duodenum. #Supply #Artery #Arteries #JS11
    Connections→Anastomoses with superior pancreaticoduodenal branches, linking coeliac and SMA circulations. #Connections #Artery #Arteries #JS11
