Tympanic membrane and ossicles
1
    Identification→Tympanic membrane (ear drum) #Identification #Membrane #MiddleEar #DS17
    Structure→Thin semitransparent membrane separating external acoustic meatus from middle ear. #Structure #Membrane #MiddleEar #DS17
    Function→Vibrates in response to sound and transmits mechanical energy to the malleus. #Function #Membrane #MiddleEar #DS17
    Innervation→External surface mainly auriculotemporal and vagal branches; internal surface mainly glossopharyngeal via tympanic plexus. #Innervation #Membrane #MiddleEar #DS17
    Clinical relevance→Perforation can cause conductive hearing loss and predispose to middle-ear infection. #Clinical #Membrane #MiddleEar #DS17
    
2
    Identification→Tympanic ring #Identification #Bone #MiddleEar #DS17
    Structure→Bony ring of temporal bone supporting much of the tympanic membrane. #Structure #Bone #MiddleEar #DS17
    Relations→Forms part of the external acoustic meatus and surrounds the tympanic membrane. #Relations #Bone #MiddleEar #DS17
    
3
    Identification→Malleus #Identification #Ossicle #MiddleEar #DS17

    a
        Identification→Head of malleus #Identification #OssiclePart #MiddleEar #DS17

    b

        Relations→Embedded in tympanic membrane and visible through it clinically. #Relations #OssiclePart #MiddleEar #DS17

    c
        Identification→Neck of malleus #Identification #OssiclePart #MiddleEar #DS17

4
    Identification→Incus #Identification #Ossicle #MiddleEar #DS17

    a
        Identification→Short limb of incus #Identification #OssiclePart #MiddleEar #DS17

        Identification→Long limb of incus #Identification #OssiclePart #MiddleEar #DS17

        Identification→Base of stapes #Identification #OssiclePart #MiddleEar #DS17

    Identification→Tensor tympani muscle #Identification #Muscle #MiddleEar #DS17

Vestibular apparatus
8
    Identification→Vestibule #Identification #InnerEar #VestibularApparatus #DS17
    Location→Central part of bony labyrinth between cochlea and semicircular canals. #Location #InnerEar #VestibularApparatus #DS17
    Contents→Contains utricle and saccule of membranous labyrinth. #Contents #InnerEar #VestibularApparatus #DS17
    Function→Detects linear acceleration and head position relative to gravity. #Function #InnerEar #VestibularApparatus #DS17
    
9
    Identification→Oval window #Identification #Opening #VestibularApparatus #DS17
    Location→Opening between middle ear and vestibule, occupied by base of stapes. #Location #Opening #VestibularApparatus #DS17
    Function→Transmits stapes vibration into perilymph of inner ear. #Function #Opening #VestibularApparatus #DS17
    
10
    Identification→Round window #Identification #Opening #VestibularApparatus #DS17
    Location→Opening from scala tympani into middle-ear region, closed by secondary tympanic membrane. #Location #Opening #VestibularApparatus #DS17
    Function→Allows pressure release/displacement of cochlear fluids during sound transmission. #Function #Opening #VestibularApparatus #DS17

Cochlea and auditory structures
14
    Identification→Cochlea #Identification #InnerEar #Cochlea #DS17
    Structure→Spiral bony labyrinth making about 2.5 turns around the modiolus. #Structure #InnerEar #Cochlea #DS17
    Function→Contains the sensory apparatus for hearing. #Function #InnerEar #Cochlea #DS17
    Compartments→Contains scala vestibuli, cochlear duct and scala tympani. #Parts #InnerEar #Cochlea #DS17
    
15
    Identification→Cochlear nerve #Identification #Nerve #Cochlea #DS17
    Roots→Peripheral processes arise from spiral ganglion neurons receiving input from hair cells of organ of Corti. #Roots #Nerve #Cochlea #DS17
    Course→Central processes form the cochlear division of CN VIII and enter brainstem at pontomedullary junction. #Course #Nerve #Cochlea #DS17
    Sensory functions→Carries auditory information from cochlea to brainstem. #Sensory #Nerve #Cochlea #DS17
    Lesion→Causes ipsilateral sensorineural hearing loss; may also produce tinnitus depending on site and pathology. #Lesion #Nerve #Cochlea #DS17
    
16
    Identification→Scala tympani #Identification #CochlearSpace #Cochlea #DS17
    Location→Perilymph-filled compartment inferior to cochlear duct. #Location #CochlearSpace #Cochlea #DS17
    Connections→Communicates with scala vestibuli at helicotrema near cochlear apex. #Connections #CochlearSpace #Cochlea #DS17
    Relations→Ends basally at round window. #Relations #CochlearSpace #Cochlea #DS17
    
17
    Identification→Scala vestibuli #Identification #CochlearSpace #Cochlea #DS17
    Location→Perilymph-filled compartment superior to cochlear duct. #Location #CochlearSpace #Cochlea #DS17
    Connections→Begins at oval window and communicates with scala tympani at helicotrema. #Connections #CochlearSpace #Cochlea #DS17
    Function→Conducts pressure waves from stapes through cochlea. #Function #CochlearSpace #Cochlea #DS17
    
18
    Identification→Cochlear duct #Identification #CochlearDuct #Cochlea #DS17
    Location→Endolymph-filled membranous duct between scala vestibuli and scala tympani. #Location #CochlearDuct #Cochlea #DS17
    Boundaries→Separated from scala vestibuli by vestibular membrane and from scala tympani by basilar membrane. #Relations #CochlearDuct #Cochlea #DS17
    Contents→Contains organ of Corti on basilar membrane. #Contents #CochlearDuct #Cochlea #DS17
    
19
    Identification→Location of the spiral organ (organ of Corti) #Identification #SensoryOrgan #Cochlea #DS17
    Location→Lies on basilar membrane within the cochlear duct. #Location #SensoryOrgan #Cochlea #DS17
    Function→Converts mechanical sound vibrations into neural signals via inner and outer hair cells. #Function #SensoryOrgan #Cochlea #DS17
    Relations→Hair-cell stereocilia interact with the overlying tectorial membrane. #Relations #SensoryOrgan #Cochlea #DS17
    Innervation→Hair cells synapse with fibres of cochlear nerve via spiral ganglion neurons. #Innervation #SensoryOrgan #Cochlea #DS17
