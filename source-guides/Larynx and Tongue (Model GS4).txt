    Location→Anterior tip of the tongue. #Location #TonguePart #Tongue #GS4

2
    Identification→Intrinsic muscles of the tongue #Identification #MuscleGroup #Tongue #GS4
    Actions→Alter the shape of the tongue: shorten, curl, flatten and narrow it. #Action #MuscleGroup #Tongue #GS4
    Innervation→Hypoglossal nerve (CN XII). #Innervation #MuscleGroup #Tongue #GS4

    Identification→Filiform papillae #Identification #Papilla #Tongue #GS4
    Structure→Most numerous, slender keratinised papillae. #Structure #Papilla #Tongue #GS4

11
    Identification→Glossopharyngeal nerve (CN IX) #Identification #Nerve #Tongue #GS4
    Roots→Arises from the medulla. #Roots #Nerve #Tongue #GS4
    Course→Exits via jugular foramen and passes toward pharynx and posterior tongue. #Course #Nerve #Tongue #GS4
    Motor functions→Supplies stylopharyngeus; carries parasympathetic secretomotor pathway to parotid. #Motor #Nerve #Tongue #GS4
    Sensory functions→General sensation and taste from posterior third of tongue; afferent limb of gag reflex. #Sensory #Nerve #Tongue #GS4
    Lesion→Causes loss of taste and sensation from posterior third of tongue, impaired afferent gag reflex, dysphagia and reduced parotid secretion. #Lesion #Nerve #Tongue #GS4
    
12
    Identification→Hypoglossal nerve (CN XII) #Identification #Nerve #Tongue #GS4
    Roots→Arises from the hypoglossal nucleus in the medulla. #Roots #Nerve #Tongue #GS4
    Course→Exits through hypoglossal canal and courses forward to the tongue. #Course #Nerve #Tongue #GS4
    Motor functions→Supplies intrinsic and extrinsic tongue muscles except palatoglossus. #Motor #Nerve #Tongue #GS4
    Sensory functions→No cutaneous sensory function. #Sensory #Nerve #Tongue #GS4
    Lesion→Lower motor neuron lesion causes ipsilateral tongue weakness, atrophy and fasciculations, with deviation toward the lesion on protrusion. #Lesion #Nerve #Tongue #GS4
    
13
    Identification→Lingual nerve #Identification #Nerve #Tongue #GS4
    Roots→Branch of mandibular division of trigeminal nerve (V3). #Roots #Nerve #Tongue #GS4
    Course→Passes through infratemporal fossa, receives chorda tympani, then runs in floor of mouth close to submandibular duct. #Course #Nerve #Tongue #GS4
    Motor functions→No branchial motor supply to tongue muscles. #Motor #Nerve #Tongue #GS4
    Sensory functions→General sensation from anterior two-thirds of tongue, floor of mouth and lingual gingiva; carries chorda tympani taste/parasympathetic fibres after they join it. #Sensory #Nerve #Tongue #GS4
    Lesion→Causes loss of general sensation from anterior two-thirds of tongue; if injured distal to chorda tympani joining, also causes loss of taste and reduced submandibular/sublingual secretion on that side. #Lesion #Nerve #Tongue #GS4
