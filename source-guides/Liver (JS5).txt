2
    Identification→Left lobe of the liver #Identification #LiverLobe #LobesAndLandmarks #JS5
    Location→Smaller anatomical lobe lying mainly to the left of the falciform ligament. #Location #LiverLobe #LobesAndLandmarks #JS5
    Relations→Related to stomach and oesophagus on its visceral surface. #Relations #LiverLobe #LobesAndLandmarks #JS5
    Function→Contains functional hepatic parenchyma with the same major metabolic and synthetic functions as the rest of the liver. #Function #LiverLobe #LobesAndLandmarks #JS5
    
3
    Identification→Quadrate lobe #Identification #LiverLobe #LobesAndLandmarks #JS5
    Location→On the visceral surface between the gallbladder fossa and fissure for the ligamentum teres. #Location #LiverLobe #LobesAndLandmarks #JS5
    Relations→Inferior to the porta hepatis. #Relations #LiverLobe #LobesAndLandmarks #JS5
    Functional anatomy→Functionally associated with the left liver because of its vascular and biliary drainage pattern. #Relations #LiverLobe #LobesAndLandmarks #JS5
    
4
    Identification→Caudate lobe #Identification #LiverLobe #LobesAndLandmarks #JS5
    Location→On posterior visceral surface between the inferior vena cava groove and fissure for ligamentum venosum. #Location #LiverLobe #LobesAndLandmarks #JS5
    Relations→Superior to the porta hepatis. #Relations #LiverLobe #LobesAndLandmarks #JS5
    Functional anatomy→Has relatively independent vascular inflow/outflow and may drain directly into the inferior vena cava. #Relations #LiverLobe #LobesAndLandmarks #JS5
    
5
    Identification→Fossa for the gallbladder #Identification #Fossa #LobesAndLandmarks #JS5
    Location→Depression on the visceral surface of the liver between right lobe and quadrate lobe. #Location #Fossa #LobesAndLandmarks #JS5
    Contents→Contains the gallbladder. #Contents #Fossa #LobesAndLandmarks #JS5
    Relations→Forms part of the main plane used to divide the liver functionally into right and left portions. #Relations #Fossa #LobesAndLandmarks #JS5
    
6
    Identification→Left liver fissure #Identification #Fissure #LobesAndLandmarks #JS5
    Location→Longitudinal fissure on visceral surface of the liver. #Location #Fissure #LobesAndLandmarks #JS5
    Contents→Anterior part contains ligamentum teres; posterior part contains ligamentum venosum. #Contents #Fissure #LobesAndLandmarks #JS5
    Function→Marks an anatomical boundary between parts of the left-sided surface structures. #Function #Fissure #LobesAndLandmarks #JS5
    
7
    Identification→Portal fissure (porta hepatis) #Identification #Hilum #LobesAndLandmarks #JS5
    Location→Transverse fissure on visceral surface between caudate and quadrate lobes. #Location #Hilum #LobesAndLandmarks #JS5
    Contents→Transmits portal vein, hepatic artery, hepatic ducts, lymphatics and autonomic nerves. #Contents #Hilum #LobesAndLandmarks #JS5
    Relations→Central hilar gateway for structures entering and leaving the liver. #Relations #Hilum #LobesAndLandmarks #JS5

    Structure→Fibrous remnant of the fetal umbilical vein. #Structure #Ligament #LobesAndLandmarks #JS5
    Location→Runs in the free inferior margin of the falciform ligament and fissure for ligamentum teres. #Location #Ligament #LobesAndLandmarks #JS5
    Embryology→Derived from the obliterated left umbilical vein. #Embryology #Ligament #LobesAndLandmarks #JS5
    
Gallbladder and biliary tree
9
    Identification→Gallbladder #Identification #Organ #BiliaryTree #JS5
    Function→Stores and concentrates bile and releases it into the biliary tree after meals. #Function #Organ #BiliaryTree #JS5
    Parts→Fundus, body and neck. #Parts #Organ #BiliaryTree #JS5
    Location→Lies in the gallbladder fossa on the visceral surface of the liver. #Location #Organ #BiliaryTree #JS5
    Drainage→Drains via the cystic duct into the common bile duct pathway. #Drainage #Organ #BiliaryTree #JS5
    Clinical relevance→Gallstones may cause biliary colic, cholecystitis or obstruction of the common bile duct. #Clinical #Organ #BiliaryTree #JS5
    
10
    Identification→Common hepatic duct #Identification #Duct #BiliaryTree #JS5
    Formation→Formed by union of right and left hepatic ducts. #Formation #Duct #BiliaryTree #JS5
    Course→Descends in the hepatoduodenal ligament and joins the cystic duct. #Course #Duct #BiliaryTree #JS5
    Termination→Joins the cystic duct to form the common bile duct. #Termination #Duct #BiliaryTree #JS5
    Function→Conducts bile from the liver toward the extrahepatic biliary tract. #Function #Duct #BiliaryTree #JS5
    
11
    Identification→Left hepatic duct #Identification #Duct #BiliaryTree #JS5
    Formation→Collects bile from the functional left side of the liver. #Formation #Duct #BiliaryTree #JS5
    Course→Runs toward the porta hepatis. #Course #Duct #BiliaryTree #JS5
    Termination→Joins the right hepatic duct to form the common hepatic duct. #Termination #Duct #BiliaryTree #JS5
    
12
    Identification→Common bile duct #Identification #Duct #BiliaryTree #JS5
    Formation→Formed by union of the common hepatic duct and cystic duct. #Formation #Duct #BiliaryTree #JS5
    Course→Descends in the hepatoduodenal ligament, passes posterior to first part of duodenum and through/behind pancreatic head region. #Course #Duct #BiliaryTree #JS5
    Termination→Usually joins the main pancreatic duct and opens at the major duodenal papilla. #Termination #Duct #BiliaryTree #JS5
    Function→Carries bile to the second part of the duodenum. #Function #Duct #BiliaryTree #JS5
    Clinical relevance→Obstruction can cause jaundice and cholangitis; distal obstruction may also affect pancreatic drainage. #Clinical #Duct #BiliaryTree #JS5
    
Vessels
13
    Identification→Hepatic artery #Identification #Artery #Vessels #JS5
    Origin→Proper hepatic artery is a branch of the common hepatic artery from the coeliac trunk. #Origin #Artery #Vessels #JS5
    Course→Ascends in the hepatoduodenal ligament within the portal triad. #Course #Artery #Vessels #JS5
    Branches→Usually divides into right and left hepatic arteries. #Branches #Artery #Vessels #JS5
    Supply→Provides oxygenated arterial blood to the liver and biliary apparatus. #Supply #Artery #Vessels #JS5
    
14
    Identification→Portal vein #Identification #Vein #Vessels #JS5
    Formation→Usually formed by union of the superior mesenteric and splenic veins behind the neck of the pancreas. #Formation #Vein #Vessels #JS5
    Course→Ascends in the hepatoduodenal ligament posterior to the hepatic artery and bile duct. #Course #Vein #Vessels #JS5
    Drainage→Carries nutrient-rich venous blood from most of the gastrointestinal tract, spleen, pancreas and gallbladder to the liver. #Drainage #Vein #Vessels #JS5
    Clinical relevance→Portal hypertension can produce oesophageal varices, ascites and portosystemic collateral enlargement. #Clinical #Vein #Vessels #JS5
    
20
    Identification→Inferior vena cava #Identification #Vein #Vessels #JS5
    Formation→Formed by union of the common iliac veins, usually at L5. #Formation #Vein #Vessels #JS5
    Course→Ascends on the right side of the vertebral column and runs in a groove on the posterior liver before passing through the diaphragm. #Course #Vein #Vessels #JS5
    Drainage→Receives hepatic veins draining blood from the liver. #Drainage #Vein #Vessels #JS5
    Relations→Closely related to the caudate lobe and posterior surface of the liver. #Relations #Vein #Vessels #JS5
