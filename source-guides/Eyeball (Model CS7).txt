    Identification→Iris #Identification #EyeStructure #CS7

16
    Identification→Optic nerve (CN II) with central retinal artery and vein #Identification #Nerve #CS7
    Roots→Optic nerve is formed by axons of retinal ganglion cells. #Roots #Nerve #CS7
    Course→Leaves the posterior globe, passes through the optic canal and reaches the optic chiasm; central retinal vessels travel within the nerve. #Course #Nerve #CS7
    Sensory functions→Vision and afferent limb of the pupillary light reflex. #Sensory #Nerve #CS7
    Contents→Central retinal artery and vein run within the optic nerve to and from the retina. #Contents #Nerve #CS7
    Lesion→Optic nerve lesion causes ipsilateral monocular visual loss and an afferent pupillary defect. #Lesion #Nerve #CS7

20
    Identification→Long and short ciliary nerves #Identification #Nerve #CS7
    Roots→Long ciliary nerves arise from nasociliary nerve (V1); short ciliary nerves arise from the ciliary ganglion. #Roots #Nerve #CS7
    Course→Pierce the sclera posteriorly and run forward between sclera and choroid. #Course #Nerve #CS7
    Sensory functions→Carry sensory fibres from the eye, particularly the cornea via long ciliary pathways. #Sensory #Nerve #CS7
    Autonomic functions→Short ciliary nerves carry parasympathetic fibres to sphincter pupillae and ciliary muscle; sympathetic fibres travel in both long and short ciliary nerves. #Autonomic #Nerve #CS7
    Lesion→Depending on which fibres are affected, may impair corneal sensation, pupillary constriction or dilation, and accommodation.    #Lesion #Nerve #CS7
