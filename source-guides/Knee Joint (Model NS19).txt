Bones
1
    Identification→Femur #Identification #Bone #Bones #NS19
    Articulations→Distally articulates with tibia and patella at the knee joint. #Articulations #Bone #Bones #NS19
    Landmarks→Medial and lateral condyles, medial and lateral epicondyles, intercondylar fossa and patellar surface. #Landmarks #Bone #Bones #NS19
    Function→Transmits body weight from hip to knee and provides attachment for major knee ligaments and muscles. #Function #Bone #Bones #NS19
    
2
    Identification→Patella #Identification #Bone #Bones #NS19
    Articulations→Articulates posteriorly with the patellar surface of the femur. #Articulations #Bone #Bones #NS19
    Structure→Largest sesamoid bone, embedded in the quadriceps tendon. #Structure #Bone #Bones #NS19
    Function→Increases the moment arm of quadriceps and protects the anterior knee joint. #Function #Bone #Bones #NS19
    Attachments→Quadriceps tendon attaches superiorly and patellar ligament inferiorly. #Attachments #Bone #Bones #NS19
    
3
    Identification→Tibia #Identification #Bone #Bones #NS19
    Articulations→Proximally articulates with femoral condyles and fibular head. #Articulations #Bone #Bones #NS19
    Landmarks→Medial and lateral condyles, intercondylar eminence and tibial tuberosity. #Landmarks #Bone #Bones #NS19
    Function→Main weight-bearing bone of the leg and distal attachment site for major knee stabilisers. #Function #Bone #Bones #NS19
    
Ligaments and menisci
4
    Identification→Patellar ligament (ligamentum patellae / patellar tendon) #Identification #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Attachments→From apex and adjacent margins of patella to tibial tuberosity. #Attachments #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Function→Continuation of quadriceps extensor mechanism, transmitting force to extend the knee. #Function #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Relations→Lies anterior to the knee joint capsule. #Relations #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Clinical relevance→Patellar tendon rupture causes loss of active knee extension and a high-riding patella may be seen. #Clinical #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    
5
    Identification→Medial meniscus #Identification #Meniscus #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Structure→C-shaped fibrocartilage attached to tibial plateau; less mobile than lateral meniscus. #Structure #Meniscus #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Attachments→Firmly attached to joint capsule and medial collateral ligament. #Attachments #Meniscus #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Function→Improves joint congruence, distributes load and absorbs shock. #Function #Meniscus #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Clinical relevance→More commonly injured than lateral meniscus, especially with twisting injuries involving valgus stress. #Clinical #Meniscus #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    
6
    Identification→Medial (tibial) collateral ligament #Identification #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Attachments→From medial femoral epicondyle to medial tibial condyle and proximal medial tibia. #Attachments #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Function→Resists valgus stress and contributes to medial knee stability. #Function #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Relations→Deep fibres blend with joint capsule and medial meniscus. #Relations #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Clinical relevance→Can be injured with valgus force; associated medial meniscal injury may occur because of their attachment. #Clinical #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    
7
    Identification→Lateral (fibular) collateral ligament #Identification #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Attachments→From lateral femoral epicondyle to head of fibula. #Attachments #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Function→Resists varus stress and stabilises lateral knee. #Function #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Relations→Cord-like and extracapsular; does not attach to lateral meniscus. #Relations #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Clinical relevance→Injury causes lateral instability and may occur with posterolateral corner injuries. #Clinical #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    
8
    Identification→Anterior cruciate ligament #Identification #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Attachments→From anterior intercondylar area of tibia to posteromedial aspect of lateral femoral condyle. #Attachments #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Function→Prevents anterior translation of tibia relative to femur and limits hyperextension/rotation. #Function #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Relations→Intracapsular but extrasynovial. #Relations #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Clinical relevance→Rupture may cause a positive Lachman/anterior drawer test and rotational instability. #Clinical #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    
9
    Identification→Posterior cruciate ligament #Identification #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Attachments→From posterior intercondylar area of tibia to lateral surface of medial femoral condyle. #Attachments #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Function→Prevents posterior translation of tibia relative to femur and limits hyperflexion. #Function #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Relations→Intracapsular but extrasynovial and generally stronger than the ACL. #Relations #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Clinical relevance→Dashboard-type trauma may rupture the PCL; posterior drawer test may be positive. #Clinical #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    
10
    Identification→Interosseous membrane #Identification #Membrane #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Attachments→Connects interosseous borders of tibia and fibula. #Attachments #Membrane #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Function→Stabilises tibiofibular relationship and provides extensive muscle attachment. #Function #Membrane #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Relations→Separates anterior and posterior compartments of the leg. #Relations #Membrane #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    
11
    Identification→Anterior ligament of the head of the fibula #Identification #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Attachments→Connects anterior aspect of fibular head to lateral tibial condyle. #Attachments #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Function→Reinforces anterior aspect of the proximal tibiofibular joint. #Function #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Relations→Part of the capsular ligamentous support of the proximal tibiofibular joint. #Relations #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    
12
    Identification→Posterior meniscofemoral ligament #Identification #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Also called→Commonly referred to as the ligament of Wrisberg when passing posterior to the PCL. #AlsoCalled #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Attachments→Runs from posterior horn of lateral meniscus to medial femoral condyle. #Attachments #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Function→Stabilises the posterior horn of the lateral meniscus and assists posterior knee stability. #Function #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Relations→Closely related to the posterior cruciate ligament. #Relations #Ligament #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    
13
    Identification→Lateral meniscus #Identification #Meniscus #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Structure→Nearly circular fibrocartilage on lateral tibial plateau and more mobile than medial meniscus. #Structure #Meniscus #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Attachments→Attached to tibial plateau and capsule; not firmly attached to fibular collateral ligament. #Attachments #Meniscus #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Function→Improves congruence, distributes load and absorbs shock. #Function #Meniscus #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Relations→Popliteus tendon separates part of the lateral meniscus from the fibular collateral ligament. #Relations #Meniscus #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
    Clinical relevance→Less commonly injured than the medial meniscus because of greater mobility. #Clinical #Meniscus #LigamentsAndMenisci #NS19
