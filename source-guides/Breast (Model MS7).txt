1
    Identification→Mammary gland lobe (pink) #Identification #MammaryGland #MS7
    Structure→One of the major subdivisions of the mammary gland, composed of multiple lobules draining toward a lactiferous duct. #Structure #MammaryGland #MS7
    Function→Contains the secretory glandular tissue responsible for milk production during lactation. #Function #MammaryGland #MS7
    Relations→Embedded within fibrofatty tissue of the breast and arranged around the nipple. #Relations #MammaryGland #MS7
    
2
    Identification→Mammary gland lobule (pink) #Identification #MammaryGland #MS7
    Structure→Small glandular unit within a mammary lobe, composed of clusters of secretory alveoli/acini. #Structure #MammaryGland #MS7
    Function→Produces milk during lactation under hormonal stimulation. #Function #MammaryGland #MS7
    Drainage→Drains into small ducts that converge into larger lactiferous ducts. #Drainage #MammaryGland #MS7
    
3
    Identification→Lactiferous duct (black) #Identification #Duct #MS7
    Function→Conveys milk from the glandular lobes toward the nipple. #Function #Duct #MS7
    Course→Runs from the mammary gland lobes toward the nipple, receiving smaller ducts from lobules. #Course #Duct #MS7
    Termination→Opens onto the surface of the nipple through a lactiferous opening. #Termination #Duct #MS7
    
4
    Identification→Lactiferous sinus (wider black part) #Identification #Duct #MS7
    Structure→Dilated portion of a lactiferous duct near the nipple. #Structure #Duct #MS7
    Function→Traditionally described as a small reservoir for milk near the nipple. #Function #Duct #MS7
    Relations→Lies immediately deep to the nipple and areolar region along the terminal part of a lactiferous duct. #Relations #Duct #MS7
    
5
    Identification→Areola #Identification #SurfaceAnatomy #MS7
    Structure→Pigmented circular area of skin surrounding the nipple. #Structure #SurfaceAnatomy #MS7
    Contents→Contains sebaceous areolar glands, including glands of Montgomery. #Contents #SurfaceAnatomy #MS7
    Function→Provides a lubricated, pigmented surface around the nipple and contributes to the nipple–areola complex during breastfeeding. #Function #SurfaceAnatomy #MS7
    
6
    Identification→Nipple (mammary papilla) #Identification #SurfaceAnatomy #MS7
    Structure→Projecting central part of the breast containing openings of the lactiferous ducts. #Structure #SurfaceAnatomy #MS7
    Function→Provides the outlet for milk during breastfeeding. #Function #SurfaceAnatomy #MS7
    Innervation→Rich sensory innervation, predominantly from anterior and lateral cutaneous branches of thoracic intercostal nerves, especially around T4. #Innervation #SurfaceAnatomy #MS7
    Relations→Located centrally within the areola. #Relations #SurfaceAnatomy #MS7
    
7
    Identification→Connective tissue and fat #Identification #BreastTissue #MS7
    Structure→Fibrous connective tissue and adipose tissue surrounding and supporting the glandular tissue. #Structure #BreastTissue #MS7
    Function→Provides structural support, shape and cushioning to the breast. #Function #BreastTissue #MS7
    Relations→Fibrous septa extend between skin and deep fascia and contribute to the suspensory ligaments of the breast. #Relations #BreastTissue #MS7
    
8
    Identification→Fat #Identification #AdiposeTissue #MS7
    Function→Contributes substantially to breast volume, contour and cushioning. #Function #AdiposeTissue #MS7
    Relations→Surrounds and separates glandular lobes and lies within the superficial fascia of the anterior chest wall. #Relations #AdiposeTissue #MS7
    Clinical relevance→The proportion of fat relative to glandular tissue varies with age, hormonal state and body composition. #Clinical #AdiposeTissue #MS7
    
9
    Identification→Pectoral fascia and retromammary space #Identification #Fascia #MS7
    Structure→Pectoral fascia covers pectoralis major; the retromammary space is a loose connective tissue plane between the breast and the deep fascia. #Structure #Fascia #MS7
    Function→The retromammary space allows the breast to move relatively freely over the pectoral fascia. #Function #Fascia #MS7
    Relations→Lies deep to the breast and superficial to pectoralis major. #Relations #Fascia #MS7
    Clinical relevance→Loss of mobility over the chest wall can occur when malignancy invades the deep fascia or pectoral structures. #Clinical #Fascia #MS7
    
10
    Identification→Pectoralis major muscle #Identification #Muscle #MS7
    Actions→Adducts and medially rotates the arm; clavicular head flexes the arm; sternocostal head extends a flexed arm. #Action #Muscle #MS7
    Innervation→Lateral and medial pectoral nerves (C5–T1). #Innervation #Muscle #MS7
    Origin→Medial half of clavicle; sternum; upper six costal cartilages; aponeurosis of external oblique. #Origin #Muscle #MS7
    Insertion→Lateral lip of the intertubercular sulcus of the humerus. #Insertion #Muscle #MS7
    
11
    Identification→Areolar glands (glands of Montgomery) #Identification #Gland #MS7
    Structure→Enlarged sebaceous glands associated with the areola. #Structure #Gland #MS7
    Function→Secrete an oily substance that lubricates and protects the nipple–areola complex, particularly during breastfeeding. #Function #Gland #MS7
    Location→Located within the skin of the areola and may form small visible elevations. #Location #Gland #MS7
    
12
    Identification→Skin #Identification #Skin #MS7
    Structure→Epidermis and dermis covering the breast, continuous with surrounding thoracic skin. #Structure #Skin #MS7
    Function→Provides a protective external barrier and carries sensory innervation. #Function #Skin #MS7
    Relations→Superficial to the glandular and fatty tissue; connected to deeper breast tissue by fibrous septa. #Relations #Skin #MS7
    Clinical relevance→Skin tethering, dimpling or peau d’orange can occur when breast malignancy affects suspensory ligaments or dermal lymphatics. #Clinical #Skin #MS7
