    Identification→Kidney #Identification #Organ #UrinarySystem #LS3_1
    Location→Retroperitoneal organ on the posterior abdominal wall; the right kidney usually lies slightly lower than the left. #Location #Organ #UrinarySystem #LS3_1
    Function→Filters blood, regulates fluid/electrolyte and acid-base balance, excretes waste and has endocrine functions including renin and erythropoietin production. #Function #Organ #UrinarySystem #LS3_1

2
    Identification→Ureter #Identification #Tube #UrinarySystem #LS3_1
    Function→Transports urine from the renal pelvis to the urinary bladder by peristalsis. #Function #Tube #UrinarySystem #LS3_1
    Course→Descends retroperitoneally from the renal pelvis, crosses the pelvic brim and enters the bladder obliquely. #Course #Tube #UrinarySystem #LS3_1
    Constrictions→Common narrowing sites are the pelvi-ureteric junction, crossing of the iliac vessels/pelvic brim, and vesico-ureteric junction. #Constrictions #Tube #UrinarySystem #LS3_1
    Clinical relevance→Ureteric calculi commonly lodge at the physiological constrictions. #Clinical #Tube #UrinarySystem #LS3_1

    g
