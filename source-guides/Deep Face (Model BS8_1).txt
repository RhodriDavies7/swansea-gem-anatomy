4
    Identification→Sphenoid bone #Identification #Bone #BS8_1
    Articulations→Articulates with frontal, parietal, temporal, occipital, ethmoid, zygomatic, vomer and palatine bones. #Articulations #Bone #BS8_1

5
    Identification→Maxilla #Identification #Bone #BS8_1
    Articulations→Articulates with multiple facial bones and contributes to the hard palate, nasal cavity and orbit. #Articulations #Bone #BS8_1
    Landmarks→Alveolar process, palatine process, infraorbital foramen and maxillary sinus. #Landmarks #Bone #BS8_1

6
    Identification→Palatine bone #Identification #Bone #BS8_1
    Articulations→Articulates with maxilla, sphenoid, ethmoid, vomer, inferior nasal concha and opposite palatine bone. #Articulations #Bone #BS8_1
    Relations→Horizontal plate forms posterior hard palate; perpendicular plate contributes to lateral nasal wall. #Relations #Bone #BS8_1
    Landmarks→Horizontal plate, perpendicular plate, pyramidal process and greater/lesser palatine foramina. #Landmarks #Bone #BS8_1
    
7
    Identification→Mandible #Identification #Bone #BS8_1
    Articulations→Articulates with the temporal bones at the temporomandibular joints. #Articulations #Bone #BS8_1
    Landmarks→Body, ramus, angle, coronoid process, condylar process, mental foramen and mandibular foramen. #Landmarks #Bone #BS8_1
    Relations→Supports lower teeth and provides major attachments for muscles of mastication and floor of mouth. #Relations #Bone #BS8_1
    
8
    Identification→Hyoid #Identification #Bone #BS8_1
    Articulations→Does not articulate directly with other bones; suspended by muscles and ligaments. #Articulations #Bone #BS8_1
    Landmarks→Body, greater horns and lesser horns. #Landmarks #Bone #BS8_1
    Relations→Located in the anterior neck between the mandible and thyroid cartilage. #Relations #Bone #BS8_1
    
9
    Identification→Lateral nasal cartilage #Identification #Cartilage #BS8_1
    Structure→Paired hyaline cartilage forming part of the upper lateral wall of the external nose. #Structure #Cartilage #BS8_1
    Relations→Lies inferior to nasal bone and superior to the major alar cartilage. #Relations #Cartilage #BS8_1

10
    Identification→Thyroid cartilage #Identification #Cartilage #BS8_1
    Structure→Largest laryngeal cartilage, formed by two laminae meeting anteriorly. #Structure #Cartilage #BS8_1
    Landmarks→Laryngeal prominence, superior/inferior horns and oblique line. #Landmarks #Cartilage #BS8_1
    Function→Protects the vocal apparatus and provides attachment for laryngeal muscles and ligaments. #Function #Cartilage #BS8_1
    
11
    Identification→Superior nasal concha #Identification #NasalConcha #BS8_1
    Structure→Curved bony projection from the ethmoid bone on the superior lateral nasal wall. #Structure #NasalConcha #BS8_1

    Relations→Overlies the superior meatus. #Relations #NasalConcha #BS8_1
    
12
    Identification→Middle nasal concha #Identification #NasalConcha #BS8_1
    Structure→Curved bony projection from the ethmoid bone on the lateral nasal wall. #Structure #NasalConcha #BS8_1
    Function→Increases mucosal surface area and influences airflow around paranasal sinus drainage pathways. #Function #NasalConcha #BS8_1
    Relations→Overlies the middle meatus. #Relations #NasalConcha #BS8_1
    
13
    Identification→Inferior nasal concha #Identification #NasalConcha #BS8_1
    Structure→Separate paired bone projecting from the inferior lateral nasal wall. #Structure #NasalConcha #BS8_1
    Function→Increases mucosal surface area and promotes warming and humidification of inspired air. #Function #NasalConcha #BS8_1
    Relations→Overlies the inferior meatus. #Relations #NasalConcha #BS8_1
    
14
    Identification→Palatine tonsil #Identification #LymphoidTissue #BS8_1
    Location→Lies in the tonsillar fossa between the palatoglossal and palatopharyngeal arches. #Location #LymphoidTissue #BS8_1
    Function→Part of Waldeyer's ring and contributes to mucosal immune surveillance. #Function #LymphoidTissue #BS8_1
    Relations→Glossopharyngeal nerve and tonsillar vessels lie close to the tonsillar bed. #Relations #LymphoidTissue #BS8_1
    
15
    Identification→Mylohyoid muscle #Identification #Muscle #BS8_1
    Actions→Elevates the floor of mouth and hyoid during swallowing; assists depression of the mandible when the hyoid is fixed. #Action #Muscle #BS8_1
    Innervation→Nerve to mylohyoid from the inferior alveolar nerve (V3). #Innervation #Muscle #BS8_1
    Origin→Mylohyoid line of mandible. #Origin #Muscle #BS8_1
    Insertion→Median mylohyoid raphe and body of hyoid. #Insertion #Muscle #BS8_1
    
16
    Identification→Geniohyoid muscle #Identification #Muscle #BS8_1
    Actions→Pulls the hyoid anterosuperiorly and shortens the floor of mouth; can assist mandibular depression. #Action #Muscle #BS8_1
    Innervation→C1 fibres travelling with the hypoglossal nerve (CN XII). #Innervation #Muscle #BS8_1
    Origin→Inferior mental spine of mandible. #Origin #Muscle #BS8_1
    Insertion→Body of hyoid. #Insertion #Muscle #BS8_1
    
17
    Identification→Sublingual gland #Identification #Gland #BS8_1
    Function→Produces predominantly mucous saliva. #Function #Gland #BS8_1
    Location→Floor of mouth beneath the mucosa, superior to mylohyoid and lateral to genioglossus. #Location #Gland #BS8_1
    Drainage→Multiple small ducts open along the sublingual fold; a larger duct may join the submandibular duct. #Drainage #Gland #BS8_1
    Innervation→Parasympathetic secretomotor fibres from facial nerve via chorda tympani, lingual nerve and submandibular ganglion. #Innervation #Gland #BS8_1
    
18
    Identification→Submandibular gland #Identification #Gland #BS8_1
    Function→Produces mixed serous and mucous saliva, predominantly serous. #Function #Gland #BS8_1
    Location→Wraps around the posterior border of mylohyoid with superficial and deep parts. #Location #Gland #BS8_1
    Drainage→Submandibular duct opens at the sublingual papilla beside the lingual frenulum. #Drainage #Gland #BS8_1
    Innervation→Parasympathetic secretomotor fibres from facial nerve via chorda tympani, lingual nerve and submandibular ganglion. #Innervation #Gland #BS8_1
    
19
    Identification→Common carotid artery #Identification #Artery #BS8_1
    Origin→Right arises from brachiocephalic trunk; left arises directly from aortic arch. #Origin #Artery #BS8_1
    Course→Ascends in the carotid sheath and usually bifurcates around the upper border of thyroid cartilage into internal and external carotid arteries. #Course #Artery #BS8_1
    Branches→No major branches in the neck before its terminal bifurcation. #Branches #Artery #BS8_1
    Supply→Supplies head and neck through the external and internal carotid systems. #Supply #Artery #BS8_1
    
20
    Identification→External carotid artery #Identification #Artery #BS8_1
    Origin→Terminal branch of common carotid artery. #Origin #Artery #BS8_1
    Course→Ascends through the carotid triangle, passes deep to posterior belly of digastric and enters parotid gland. #Course #Artery #BS8_1
    Branches→Superior thyroid, ascending pharyngeal, lingual, facial, occipital, posterior auricular, maxillary and superficial temporal arteries. #Branches #Artery #BS8_1
    Supply→Most extracranial structures of head and neck. #Supply #Artery #BS8_1
    
21
    Identification→Internal carotid artery #Identification #Artery #BS8_1
    Origin→Terminal branch of common carotid artery. #Origin #Artery #BS8_1
    Course→Ascends in carotid sheath without branching in the neck, enters carotid canal and continues intracranially. #Course #Artery #BS8_1
    Supply→Major arterial supply to the brain and orbit. #Supply #Artery #BS8_1
    Relations→Lies posterolateral to the external carotid near the bifurcation before ascending deep in the neck. #Relations #Artery #BS8_1
    
22
    Identification→Greater palatine artery #Identification #Artery #BS8_1
    Origin→Terminal branch of the descending palatine artery from the maxillary artery. #Origin #Artery #BS8_1
    Course→Descends through the greater palatine canal, emerges at the greater palatine foramen and runs anteriorly on the hard palate. #Course #Artery #BS8_1
    Supply→Hard palate, palatal gingiva and adjacent structures. #Supply #Artery #BS8_1
    Relations→Anastomoses anteriorly with the septal branch of the sphenopalatine artery via incisive canal region. #Relations #Artery #BS8_1
    
23
    Identification→Optic nerve #Identification #Nerve #BS8_1
    Roots→Special sensory fibres from retinal ganglion cells; functionally part of the CNS. #Roots #Nerve #BS8_1
    Course→Leaves the orbit through the optic canal and reaches the optic chiasm. #Course #Nerve #BS8_1
    Sensory functions→Vision. #Sensory #Nerve #BS8_1
    Lesion→Lesion causes ipsilateral monocular visual loss and an afferent pupillary defect. #Lesion #Nerve #BS8_1
    
24
    Identification→Trigeminal nerve #Identification #Nerve #BS8_1
    Roots→Large sensory root and smaller motor root emerging from the lateral pons. #Roots #Nerve #BS8_1
    Course→Sensory root expands into the trigeminal ganglion; V1, V2 and V3 then leave the cranial cavity by different foramina. #Course #Nerve #BS8_1
    Motor functions→Motor fibres travel with V3 to muscles of mastication and selected first-pharyngeal-arch muscles. #Motor #Nerve #BS8_1
    Sensory functions→Major somatic sensory nerve of face, oral/nasal cavities, teeth, cornea and much of cranial dura. #Sensory #Nerve #BS8_1
    Lesion→May cause ipsilateral facial sensory loss; motor root involvement causes weakness of mastication with the jaw deviating toward the side of the lesion on opening; the corneal reflex afferent limb may be impaired if V1 is involved.    #Lesion #Nerve #BS8_1
    
25
    Identification→Trigeminal ganglion #Identification #Ganglion #BS8_1
    Location→Lies in Meckel's cave near the apex of the petrous temporal bone. #Location #Ganglion #BS8_1
    Function→Contains sensory neuron cell bodies of the trigeminal nerve. #Function #Ganglion #BS8_1
    Branches→Gives rise to ophthalmic (V1), maxillary (V2) and mandibular (V3) divisions. #Branches #Ganglion #BS8_1
    
26
    Identification→Ophthalmic nerve #Identification #Nerve #BS8_1
    Course→Passes in the lateral wall of the cavernous sinus and exits through the superior orbital fissure. #Course #Nerve #BS8_1
    Sensory functions→Supplies cornea, upper eyelid, forehead, scalp, dorsum of nose and parts of nasal cavity and cranial dura. #Sensory #Nerve #BS8_1
    Motor functions→None; purely sensory. #Motor #Nerve #BS8_1
    Lesion→Causes sensory loss over the forehead, cornea and dorsum of the nose, with loss or reduction of the afferent limb of the corneal reflex.    #Lesion #Nerve #BS8_1
    
27
    Identification→Maxillary nerve #Identification #Nerve #BS8_1
    Course→Passes in the lateral wall of the cavernous sinus, exits via foramen rotundum and enters the pterygopalatine fossa. #Course #Nerve #BS8_1
    Sensory functions→Supplies midface, upper lip, maxillary teeth, palate and parts of nasal cavity. #Sensory #Nerve #BS8_1
    Motor functions→None; purely sensory. #Motor #Nerve #BS8_1
    Lesion→Causes sensory loss or paraesthesia over the midface, upper lip, maxillary teeth and palate; there is no motor deficit because V2 is purely sensory.    #Lesion #Nerve #BS8_1
    
28
    Identification→Mandibular nerve #Identification #Nerve #BS8_1
    Course→Exits cranial cavity through foramen ovale and enters the infratemporal fossa. #Course #Nerve #BS8_1
    Motor functions→Supplies muscles of mastication, mylohyoid, anterior belly of digastric, tensor tympani and tensor veli palatini. #Motor #Nerve #BS8_1
    Sensory functions→Supplies lower face, mandibular teeth, anterior two-thirds of tongue for general sensation and parts of external ear and dura. #Sensory #Nerve #BS8_1
    Lesion→Causes sensory loss over the lower face, mandibular teeth and general sensation from the anterior two-thirds of the tongue; motor involvement weakens mastication and the jaw deviates toward the affected side on opening.    #Lesion #Nerve #BS8_1
    
29
    Identification→Olfactory nerves #Identification #Nerve #BS8_1
    Roots→Special sensory fibres from olfactory receptor neurons in the olfactory epithelium. #Roots #Nerve #BS8_1
    Course→Small fascicles pass through foramina in the cribriform plate to the olfactory bulb. #Course #Nerve #BS8_1
    Sensory functions→Smell. #Sensory #Nerve #BS8_1
    Lesion→Injury may cause anosmia and can occur with cribriform plate fractures. #Lesion #Nerve #BS8_1
    
30
    Identification→Palatine nerves #Identification #Nerve #BS8_1
    Roots→Branches associated with the maxillary nerve via the pterygopalatine ganglion. #Roots #Nerve #BS8_1
    Course→Descend through the palatine canals to the palate as greater and lesser palatine nerves. #Course #Nerve #BS8_1
    Sensory functions→General sensation from much of the hard and soft palate. #Sensory #Nerve #BS8_1
    Parasympathetic functions→Carry postganglionic parasympathetic secretomotor fibres to palatal glands. #Parasympathetic #Nerve #BS8_1

32
    Identification→Greater palatine nerve #Identification #Nerve #BS8_1
    Roots→Branch of maxillary nerve distribution via the pterygopalatine ganglion. #Roots #Nerve #BS8_1
    Course→Descends through greater palatine canal and emerges through greater palatine foramen to run anteriorly on hard palate. #Course #Nerve #BS8_1
    Sensory functions→Supplies most of the hard palate and palatal gingiva. #Sensory #Nerve #BS8_1

33
    Identification→Nasopalatine nerve #Identification #Nerve #BS8_1
    Roots→Branch of maxillary nerve distribution via the pterygopalatine ganglion. #Roots #Nerve #BS8_1
    Course→Enters nasal cavity through sphenopalatine foramen, crosses nasal septum and passes through incisive canal. #Course #Nerve #BS8_1

34
    Identification→Lingual nerve #Identification #Nerve #BS8_1
    Roots→Branch of posterior division of mandibular nerve (V3). #Roots #Nerve #BS8_1
    Course→Descends in infratemporal fossa, passes close to mandibular third molar, then runs in floor of mouth around the submandibular duct. #Course #Nerve #BS8_1
    Sensory functions→General sensation from anterior two-thirds of tongue, floor of mouth and lingual gingiva. #Sensory #Nerve #BS8_1
    Parasympathetic functions→Carries chorda tympani taste fibres and preganglionic parasympathetic fibres to the submandibular ganglion. #Parasympathetic #Nerve #BS8_1
    Lesion→Can cause loss of general sensation and taste from anterior tongue and reduced salivary secretion depending on lesion site. #Lesion #Nerve #BS8_1
    
35
    Identification→Pterygopalatine ganglion #Identification #Ganglion #BS8_1
    Location→Lies in the pterygopalatine fossa, suspended from the maxillary nerve. #Location #Ganglion #BS8_1
    Function→Parasympathetic ganglion for lacrimal, nasal and palatal gland secretomotor pathways. #Function #Ganglion #BS8_1
    Roots→Preganglionic parasympathetic fibres arrive via greater petrosal nerve and nerve of pterygoid canal; sympathetic and sensory fibres pass through without synapsing. #Roots #Ganglion #BS8_1
    Branches→Distributes fibres through branches of V2, including palatine and nasal branches. #Branches #Ganglion #BS8_1
    
36
    Identification→Vagus nerve #Identification #Nerve #BS8_1
    Roots→Medullary nuclei including nucleus ambiguus, dorsal motor nucleus of vagus and nucleus solitarius pathways. #Roots #Nerve #BS8_1
    Course→Emerges from postolivary sulcus, exits through jugular foramen and descends in the carotid sheath. #Course #Nerve #BS8_1
    Motor functions→Supplies most muscles of the palate, pharynx and larynx. #Motor #Nerve #BS8_1
    Sensory functions→Visceral sensation from thoracoabdominal organs and sensation from larynx/pharynx; taste from epiglottic region. #Sensory #Nerve #BS8_1
    Parasympathetic functions→Provides parasympathetic supply to thoracic and much of abdominal viscera. #Parasympathetic #Nerve #BS8_1
    Note→May have fallen off the physical model. #ModelNote #Nerve #BS8_1
    Lesion→May cause dysphagia and hoarseness, ipsilateral palatal weakness with the uvula deviating away from the lesion, and loss of the efferent limb of the gag reflex.    #Lesion #Nerve #BS8_1
    
37
    Identification→Hypoglossal nerve #Identification #Nerve #BS8_1
    Roots→Hypoglossal nucleus in the medulla. #Roots #Nerve #BS8_1
    Course→Emerges between pyramid and olive, exits via hypoglossal canal and passes toward the tongue. #Course #Nerve #BS8_1
    Motor functions→Supplies intrinsic and extrinsic tongue muscles except palatoglossus. #Motor #Nerve #BS8_1
