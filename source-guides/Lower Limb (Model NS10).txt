        Identification→Internal iliac artery #Identification #Artery #NS10
        Origin→Common iliac artery near sacroiliac joint. #Origin #Artery #NS10
        Course→Descends into lesser pelvis; usually divides into anterior and posterior divisions. #Course #Artery #NS10
        Branches→Posterior division: iliolumbar, lateral sacral, superior gluteal. Anterior division commonly gives obturator, inferior gluteal, internal pudendal, umbilical/superior vesical, middle rectal and sex-dependent visceral branches. #Branches #Artery #NS10
        Supply→Pelvic walls and viscera, gluteal region, perineum and part of medial thigh. #Supply #Artery #NS10
        Relations→Branching is variable; lies anterior to the internal iliac vein. #Relations #Artery #NS10
