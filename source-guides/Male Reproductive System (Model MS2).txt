7
    Identification→Seminal vesicles #Identification #Organ #MS2

8
    Identification→Ductus deferens #Identification #Tube #MS2

9
    Identification→Ureter #Identification #Tube #MS2
    Function→Transports urine from renal pelvis to urinary bladder by peristalsis. #Function #Tube #MS2
    Course→Descends retroperitoneally on psoas, crosses the pelvic brim near the iliac vessels, then runs on the lateral pelvic wall and enters the bladder obliquely. #Course #Tube #MS2

    Innervation→Visceral afferents travel mainly with sympathetic fibres to T11–L2 spinal segments; pain may refer from loin to groin. #Innervation #Tube #MS2
