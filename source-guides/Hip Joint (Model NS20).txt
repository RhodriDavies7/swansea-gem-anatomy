Bones and bony landmarks
1
    Identification→Hip bone (os coxae) #Identification #Bone #Bones #NS20
    Components→Formed by the ilium, pubis and ischium, which fuse around the acetabulum. #Components #Bone #Bones #NS20
    Function→Transmits body weight from axial skeleton to lower limb and forms the acetabulum. #Function #Bone #Bones #NS20
    a
        Identification→Ilium #Identification #Bone #Bones #NS20
        Articulations→Articulates posteriorly with the sacrum and contributes superiorly to the acetabulum. #Articulations #Bone #Bones #NS20
        Landmarks→Iliac crest, ASIS, AIIS, PSIS, PIIS, iliac fossa and greater sciatic notch. #Landmarks #Bone #Bones #NS20
        Function→Broad superior part of hip bone transmitting load through the sacroiliac joint. #Function #Bone #Bones #NS20
    b
        Identification→Pubis #Identification #Bone #Bones #NS20
        Articulations→Articulates with opposite pubis at pubic symphysis and contributes to acetabulum and obturator foramen. #Articulations #Bone #Bones #NS20
        Landmarks→Body, superior ramus, inferior ramus, pubic crest and pubic tubercle. #Landmarks #Bone #Bones #NS20
    c
        Identification→Ischium #Identification #Bone #Bones #NS20
        Articulations→Contributes posteroinferiorly to acetabulum and joins pubis anteriorly. #Articulations #Bone #Bones #NS20
        Landmarks→Ischial spine, ischial tuberosity, lesser sciatic notch and ramus. #Landmarks #Bone #Bones #NS20
        
2
    Identification→Femur #Identification #Bone #Bones #NS20
    Articulations→Articulates proximally with acetabulum and distally with tibia and patella. #Articulations #Bone #Bones #NS20
    Function→Transmits body weight and acts as the major lever of the thigh. #Function #Bone #Bones #NS20
    d
        Identification→Head of femur #Identification #BonyLandmark #Bones #NS20
        Articulation→Articulates with lunate surface of acetabulum. #Articulations #BonyLandmark #Bones #NS20
        Relations→Covered by articular cartilage except at fovea capitis. #Relations #BonyLandmark #Bones #NS20
        Blood supply→Mainly retinacular branches of medial circumflex femoral artery; artery in ligament of head is usually minor in adults. #BloodSupply #BonyLandmark #Bones #NS20
        Clinical relevance→Intracapsular femoral-neck fractures may disrupt retinacular vessels and cause avascular necrosis. #Clinical #BonyLandmark #Bones #NS20
    e
        Identification→Greater trochanter #Identification #BonyLandmark #Bones #NS20
        Attachments→Major attachment site for gluteus medius/minimus, piriformis, obturator internus and gemelli. #Attachments #BonyLandmark #Bones #NS20
        Relations→Large lateral projection of proximal femur. #Relations #BonyLandmark #Bones #NS20
    f
        Identification→Lesser trochanter #Identification #BonyLandmark #Bones #NS20
        Attachments→Insertion of iliopsoas tendon. #Attachments #BonyLandmark #Bones #NS20
        Relations→Posteromedial projection of proximal femur. #Relations #BonyLandmark #Bones #NS20
        
Ligaments and joint structures
3
    Identification→Iliofemoral ligament #Identification #Ligament #LigamentsAndJoint #NS20
    Attachments→From anterior inferior iliac spine and adjacent acetabular rim to intertrochanteric line of femur. #Attachments #Ligament #LigamentsAndJoint #NS20
    Function→Strongly resists hyperextension of the hip and helps maintain upright posture with minimal muscular effort. #Function #Ligament #LigamentsAndJoint #NS20
    Structure→Y-shaped thickening of anterior joint capsule; one of the strongest ligaments in the body. #Structure #Ligament #LigamentsAndJoint #NS20
    Clinical relevance→Its tension in extension contributes to stability of the standing hip. #Clinical #Ligament #LigamentsAndJoint #NS20
    
4
    Identification→Ischiofemoral ligament #Identification #Ligament #LigamentsAndJoint #NS20
    Attachments→From ischial part of acetabular rim to posterior femoral neck/greater trochanter region. #Attachments #Ligament #LigamentsAndJoint #NS20
    Function→Reinforces posterior capsule and limits extension and internal rotation. #Function #Ligament #LigamentsAndJoint #NS20
    Relations→Fibres spiral superolaterally around the femoral neck. #Relations #Ligament #LigamentsAndJoint #NS20
    
5
    Identification→Pubofemoral ligament #Identification #Ligament #LigamentsAndJoint #NS20
    Attachments→From pubic part of acetabular rim and superior pubic ramus to inferior capsule/intertrochanteric region. #Attachments #Ligament #LigamentsAndJoint #NS20
    Function→Limits excessive abduction and extension of the hip. #Function #Ligament #LigamentsAndJoint #NS20
    Relations→Reinforces the inferomedial part of the joint capsule. #Relations #Ligament #LigamentsAndJoint #NS20
    
6
    Identification→Acetabular labrum #Identification #Fibrocartilage #LigamentsAndJoint #NS20
    Structure→Fibrocartilaginous rim attached to margin of acetabulum. #Structure #Fibrocartilage #LigamentsAndJoint #NS20
    Function→Deepens acetabular socket and improves hip-joint stability and seal. #Function #Fibrocartilage #LigamentsAndJoint #NS20
    Relations→Continuous inferiorly with transverse acetabular ligament across acetabular notch. #Relations #Fibrocartilage #LigamentsAndJoint #NS20
    Clinical relevance→Labral tears can cause groin pain, clicking and mechanical symptoms. #Clinical #Fibrocartilage #LigamentsAndJoint #NS20
    
7
    Identification→Orbicular zone of the fibrous joint capsule #Identification #Capsule #LigamentsAndJoint #NS20
    Structure→Circular fibres of the fibrous capsule that encircle the femoral neck. #Structure #Capsule #LigamentsAndJoint #NS20
    Function→Helps retain the femoral head within the acetabulum and resists distraction. #Function #Capsule #LigamentsAndJoint #NS20
    Relations→Deep circular component of the hip-joint capsule. #Relations #Capsule #LigamentsAndJoint #NS20
    
8
    Identification→Ligament of head of the femur (round ligament; ligamentum teres) #Identification #Ligament #LigamentsAndJoint #NS20
    Attachments→From fovea capitis of femoral head to margins of acetabular notch and transverse acetabular ligament. #Attachments #Ligament #LigamentsAndJoint #NS20
    Function→Provides limited mechanical stability and carries a small artery to the femoral head. #Function #Ligament #LigamentsAndJoint #NS20
    Blood supply→Carries the acetabular branch of obturator artery, particularly important in children. #BloodSupply #Ligament #LigamentsAndJoint #NS20
    Clinical relevance→Can be torn during traumatic hip dislocation. #Clinical #Ligament #LigamentsAndJoint #NS20
    
9
    Identification→Obturator membrane #Identification #Membrane #LigamentsAndJoint #NS20
    Attachments→Attaches around margins of obturator foramen, leaving the obturator canal superiorly. #Attachments #Membrane #LigamentsAndJoint #NS20
    Function→Closes most of obturator foramen and provides attachment for obturator muscles. #Function #Membrane #LigamentsAndJoint #NS20
    Contents→Obturator nerve and vessels pass through the obturator canal. #Contents #Membrane #LigamentsAndJoint #NS20
    Relations→Obturator internus arises from its pelvic surface and obturator externus from its external surface. #Relations #Membrane #LigamentsAndJoint #NS20
