A. External ear

1
    Identification→External acoustic meatus (external auditory canal) #Identification #Canal #ExternalEar #DS3
    Course→Extends from the auricle to the tympanic membrane; lateral part is cartilaginous and medial part bony. #Course #Canal #ExternalEar #DS3
    Function→Conducts sound waves toward the tympanic membrane. #Function #Canal #ExternalEar #DS3
    Relations→Ends medially at the tympanic membrane. #Relations #Canal #ExternalEar #DS3
    
2
    Identification→Tympanic membrane (ear drum) #Identification #Membrane #ExternalEar #DS3
    Structure→Thin membrane separating the external acoustic meatus from the tympanic cavity. #Structure #Membrane #ExternalEar #DS3
    Function→Vibrates in response to sound and transmits movement to the ossicles via the malleus. #Function #Membrane #ExternalEar #DS3
    Relations→Handle of the malleus is attached to its medial surface. #Relations #Membrane #ExternalEar #DS3
    
3
    Identification→Tympanic ring #Identification #FibrousRing #ExternalEar #DS3
    Structure→Fibrocartilaginous ring supporting and anchoring most of the tympanic membrane. #Structure #FibrousRing #ExternalEar #DS3
    Function→Fixes the tympanic membrane at its periphery within the tympanic sulcus. #Function #FibrousRing #ExternalEar #DS3
    
B. Middle ear

4
    Identification→Tympanic cavity #Identification #Cavity #MiddleEar #DS3
    Structure→Air-filled cavity within the petrous temporal bone containing the auditory ossicles. #Structure #Cavity #MiddleEar #DS3
    Contents→Malleus, incus, stapes, tensor tympani, stapedius, chorda tympani and related vessels/nerves. #Contents #Cavity #MiddleEar #DS3
    Connections→Communicates anteriorly with the pharyngotympanic tube and posteriorly with mastoid air cells via the mastoid antrum. #Connections #Cavity #MiddleEar #DS3
    
5
    Identification→Opening of the pharyngotympanic tube in the tympanic cavity #Identification #Opening #MiddleEar #DS3
    Location→Anterior wall of the tympanic cavity. #Location #Opening #MiddleEar #DS3
    Function→Connects the middle ear to the pharyngotympanic tube for pressure equalisation and drainage. #Function #Opening #MiddleEar #DS3
    
6
    Identification→Opening of the pharyngotympanic tube in the nasopharynx #Identification #Opening #MiddleEar #DS3
    Location→Lateral wall of the nasopharynx. #Location #Opening #MiddleEar #DS3
    Function→Permits communication between the nasopharynx and middle ear to equalise pressure across the tympanic membrane. #Function #Opening #MiddleEar #DS3
    
7
    Identification→Malleus #Identification #Ossicle #MiddleEar #DS3
    Articulations→Articulates with the incus at the incudomalleolar joint. #Articulations #Ossicle #MiddleEar #DS3
    Function→Transmits vibration from the tympanic membrane to the incus. #Function #Ossicle #MiddleEar #DS3
    Relations→Handle is attached to the tympanic membrane. #Relations #Ossicle #MiddleEar #DS3
    a
        Identification→Head of malleus #Identification #OssiclePart #MiddleEar #DS3
        Relations→Lies in the epitympanic recess and articulates with the body of the incus. #Relations #OssiclePart #MiddleEar #DS3
    b
        Identification→Handle of malleus #Identification #OssiclePart #MiddleEar #DS3
        Relations→Embedded in the tympanic membrane and transmits its vibration to the malleus. #Relations #OssiclePart #MiddleEar #DS3
    c
        Identification→Anterior process of malleus #Identification #OssiclePart #MiddleEar #DS3
        Relations→Slender process projecting anteriorly toward the petrotympanic fissure and related malleolar ligaments. #Relations #OssiclePart #MiddleEar #DS3
        
8
    Identification→Incus #Identification #Ossicle #MiddleEar #DS3
    Articulations→Articulates with malleus proximally and stapes distally via the lenticular process. #Articulations #Ossicle #MiddleEar #DS3
    Function→Transmits vibration from malleus to stapes. #Function #Ossicle #MiddleEar #DS3
    a
        Identification→Short limb of incus #Identification #OssiclePart #MiddleEar #DS3
        Relations→Projects posteriorly and is supported in the fossa incudis. #Relations #OssiclePart #MiddleEar #DS3
    b
        Identification→Long limb of incus #Identification #OssiclePart #MiddleEar #DS3
        Relations→Descends toward the stapes and ends in the lenticular process. #Relations #OssiclePart #MiddleEar #DS3
        
9
    Identification→Stapes #Identification #Ossicle #MiddleEar #DS3
    Articulations→Articulates with the lenticular process of incus; base occupies the oval window. #Articulations #Ossicle #MiddleEar #DS3
    Function→Transmits ossicular vibration into the fluid of the inner ear at the oval window. #Function #Ossicle #MiddleEar #DS3
    a
        Identification→Head of stapes #Identification #OssiclePart #MiddleEar #DS3
        Relations→Articulates with the lenticular process of the incus. #Relations #OssiclePart #MiddleEar #DS3
    b
        Identification→Limb (crus) of stapes #Identification #OssiclePart #MiddleEar #DS3
        Relations→Connects the stapes head to the base; the stapes has anterior and posterior crura. #Relations #OssiclePart #MiddleEar #DS3
    c
        Identification→Base of stapes #Identification #OssiclePart #MiddleEar #DS3
        Relations→Fits into the oval window and transmits vibration to perilymph of the vestibule. #Relations #OssiclePart #MiddleEar #DS3
        
10
    Identification→Lenticular process of incus #Identification #OssiclePart #MiddleEar #DS3
    Relations→Small terminal process of the long limb of incus that articulates with the head of the stapes. #Relations #OssiclePart #MiddleEar #DS3
    Function→Transfers vibration from incus to stapes. #Function #OssiclePart #MiddleEar #DS3
    
11
    Identification→Chorda tympani #Identification #Nerve #MiddleEar #DS3
    Roots→Branch of the facial nerve (CN VII). #Roots #Nerve #MiddleEar #DS3
    Course→Crosses the tympanic cavity between the malleus and incus, exits via petrotympanic fissure and joins the lingual nerve. #Course #Nerve #MiddleEar #DS3
    Sensory functions→Carries taste from the anterior two-thirds of the tongue. #Sensory #Nerve #MiddleEar #DS3
    Parasympathetic functions→Carries preganglionic fibres to the submandibular ganglion for submandibular and sublingual glands. #Parasympathetic #Nerve #MiddleEar #DS3
    Lesion→Causes loss or reduction of taste from the anterior two-thirds of the tongue and reduced submandibular and sublingual salivary secretion; general tongue sensation is preserved if the lingual nerve itself is intact.     #Lesion #Nerve #MiddleEar #DS3
    
12
    Identification→Tensor tympani muscle #Identification #Muscle #MiddleEar #DS3
    Actions→Tenses the tympanic membrane by pulling the malleus medially, reducing excessive ossicular movement. #Action #Muscle #MiddleEar #DS3
    Innervation→Mandibular division of trigeminal nerve (V3), via nerve to medial pterygoid/tensor tympani branch. #Innervation #Muscle #MiddleEar #DS3
    Origin→Cartilaginous pharyngotympanic tube and adjacent sphenoid/petrous temporal bone. #Origin #Muscle #MiddleEar #DS3
    Insertion→Handle of malleus. #Insertion #Muscle #MiddleEar #DS3
    
C. Inner ear

13
    Identification→Vestibule #Identification #InnerEarStructure #InnerEar #DS3
    Structure→Central part of the bony labyrinth between cochlea and semicircular canals. #Structure #InnerEarStructure #InnerEar #DS3
    Contents→Contains the utricle and saccule of the membranous labyrinth. #Contents #InnerEarStructure #InnerEar #DS3
    Function→Participates in static equilibrium and linear acceleration sensing through the utricle and saccule. #Function #InnerEarStructure #InnerEar #DS3
    
14
    Identification→Oval window (covered by the base of the stapes bone) #Identification #Opening #InnerEar #DS3
    Location→Opening in the medial wall of the middle ear leading to the vestibule. #Location #Opening #InnerEar #DS3
    Relations→Occupied by the base of the stapes. #Relations #Opening #InnerEar #DS3
    Function→Receives mechanical vibration from the stapes and transmits it into inner-ear perilymph. #Function #Opening #InnerEar #DS3
    
15
    Identification→Cochlear promontory #Identification #BonyLandmark #InnerEar #DS3
    Location→Rounded prominence on the medial wall of the tympanic cavity produced by the basal turn of the cochlea. #Location #BonyLandmark #InnerEar #DS3
    Relations→Lies between the oval and round window regions. #Relations #BonyLandmark #InnerEar #DS3
    
16
    Identification→Round window #Identification #Opening #InnerEar #DS3
    Location→Opening between middle ear and scala tympani, closed by the secondary tympanic membrane. #Location #Opening #InnerEar #DS3
    Function→Allows pressure release/displacement of cochlear fluid when the stapes moves at the oval window. #Function #Opening #InnerEar #DS3
    
17
    Identification→Lateral semicircular canal #Identification #SemicircularCanal #InnerEar #DS3
    Function→Detects angular acceleration primarily in the horizontal plane. #Function #SemicircularCanal #InnerEar #DS3
    Connections→Opens into the vestibule at its ends, with an ampullary enlargement at one end. #Connections #SemicircularCanal #InnerEar #DS3
    a
        Identification→Lateral bony ampulla #Identification #Ampulla #InnerEar #DS3
        Function→Enlarged bony end housing the ampulla of the lateral semicircular duct and its sensory crista. #Function #Ampulla #InnerEar #DS3
        
18
    Identification→Anterior semicircular canal #Identification #SemicircularCanal #InnerEar #DS3
    Function→Detects angular acceleration in a vertical plane. #Function #SemicircularCanal #InnerEar #DS3
    Relations→Shares a common crus with the posterior semicircular canal before entering the vestibule. #Relations #SemicircularCanal #InnerEar #DS3
    b
        Identification→Anterior bony ampulla #Identification #Ampulla #InnerEar #DS3
        Function→Enlarged end housing the ampulla of the anterior semicircular duct and its sensory crista. #Function #Ampulla #InnerEar #DS3
        
19
    Identification→Posterior semicircular canal #Identification #SemicircularCanal #InnerEar #DS3
    Function→Detects angular acceleration in a vertical plane. #Function #SemicircularCanal #InnerEar #DS3
    Relations→Shares a common crus with the anterior semicircular canal. #Relations #SemicircularCanal #InnerEar #DS3
    c
        Identification→Posterior bony ampulla #Identification #Ampulla #InnerEar #DS3
        Function→Enlarged end housing the ampulla of the posterior semicircular duct and its sensory crista. #Function #Ampulla #InnerEar #DS3
        
20
    Identification→Cochlea #Identification #InnerEarStructure #InnerEar #DS3
    Structure→Spiral part of the bony labyrinth surrounding the modiolus and containing the cochlear duct between scala vestibuli and scala tympani. #Structure #InnerEarStructure #InnerEar #DS3
    Function→Converts sound-induced mechanical vibrations into neural signals for hearing. #Function #InnerEarStructure #InnerEar #DS3
    Relations→Basal turn lies close to the middle-ear medial wall and creates the cochlear promontory. #Relations #InnerEarStructure #InnerEar #DS3
    
21
    Identification→Vestibulocochlear nerve (CN VIII) #Identification #Nerve #InnerEar #DS3
    Roots→Vestibular and cochlear divisions from the inner ear. #Roots #Nerve #InnerEar #DS3
    Course→Passes through the internal acoustic meatus and enters the brainstem at the cerebellopontine angle. #Course #Nerve #InnerEar #DS3
    Sensory functions→Hearing and balance. #Sensory #Nerve #InnerEar #DS3
    Lesion→Can cause sensorineural hearing loss, tinnitus, vertigo and imbalance. #Lesion #Nerve #InnerEar #DS3
