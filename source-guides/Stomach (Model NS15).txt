Gastric wall and muscle layers
6
    Identification→Tunica muscularis #Identification #MuscleLayer #GastricWall #JS4
    Layers→Outer longitudinal, middle circular and inner oblique smooth muscle layers. #Layers #MuscleLayer #GastricWall #JS4
    Function→Mixes and propels gastric contents by peristaltic contractions. #Function #MuscleLayer #GastricWall #JS4
    
7
    Identification→Longitudinal muscle fibres #Identification #MuscleLayer #GastricWall #JS4
    Location→Outermost layer of the muscularis externa. #Location #MuscleLayer #GastricWall #JS4
    Function→Shortens the stomach wall and contributes to peristalsis. #Function #MuscleLayer #GastricWall #JS4
    
8
    Identification→Circular muscular fibres #Identification #MuscleLayer #GastricWall #JS4
    Location→Middle layer of the muscularis externa. #Location #MuscleLayer #GastricWall #JS4
    Function→Constricts the stomach lumen during mixing and peristalsis. #Function #MuscleLayer #GastricWall #JS4

9
    Identification→Oblique muscular fibres #Identification #MuscleLayer #GastricWall #JS4
    Location→Innermost muscle layer, most prominent in the body/fundus. #Location #MuscleLayer #GastricWall #JS4
    Function→Adds powerful churning and mixing movements unique to the stomach. #Function #MuscleLayer #GastricWall #JS4
    
10
    Identification→Peritoneum #Identification #Serosa #GastricWall #JS4

Vagal innervation
15
    Identification→Left vagus nerve #Identification #Nerve #VagalInnervation #JS4
    Roots→Vagus nerve (CN X). #Roots #Nerve #VagalInnervation #JS4
    Course→Oesophageal plexus fibres reorganise so the left vagus mainly forms the anterior vagal trunk. #Course #Nerve #VagalInnervation #JS4
    Motor functions→Parasympathetic supply increases gastric motility and promotes relaxation/coordination of gastric sphincter mechanisms. #Motor #Nerve #VagalInnervation #JS4
    Sensory functions→Carries visceral afferent fibres involved in physiological reflexes from the foregut. #Sensory #Nerve #VagalInnervation #JS4
    Lesion→Vagal interruption reduces parasympathetic stimulation of gastric motility and secretion; effects depend on lesion level and whether both trunks are involved. #Lesion #Nerve #VagalInnervation #JS4
