    Identification→Thyroid cartilage #Identification #Cartilage #CartilagesAndBones #GS7

3
    Identification→Cricoid cartilage #Identification #Cartilage #CartilagesAndBones #GS7
    Structure→Only complete cartilaginous ring of the airway; narrow anterior arch and broad posterior lamina. #Structure #Cartilage #CartilagesAndBones #GS7
    Function→Maintains airway patency and supports the arytenoid cartilages. #Function #Cartilage #CartilagesAndBones #GS7
    Relations→Inferior to thyroid cartilage and superior to the first tracheal ring. #Relations #Cartilage #CartilagesAndBones #GS7
    Clinical relevance→Cricoid cartilage lies around C6, where the larynx continues as the trachea. #Clinical #Cartilage #CartilagesAndBones #GS7
    
4
    Identification→Arytenoid cartilage #Identification #Cartilage #CartilagesAndBones #GS7
    Structure→Paired pyramidal cartilages sitting on the superior border of the cricoid lamina. #Structure #Cartilage #CartilagesAndBones #GS7
    Landmarks→Each has a vocal process anteriorly and muscular process laterally. #Landmarks #Cartilage #CartilagesAndBones #GS7
    Function→Rotates and glides to abduct/adduct and tension the vocal folds. #Function #Cartilage #CartilagesAndBones #GS7
    
5

    Relations→Lie within the aryepiglottic folds. #Relations #Cartilage #CartilagesAndBones #GS7
    Function→Support the posterior aryepiglottic folds. #Function #Cartilage #CartilagesAndBones #GS7
    
6
    Identification→Cuneiform cartilage (cartilage of Wrisberg) #Identification #Cartilage #CartilagesAndBones #GS7
    Structure→Small paired elastic cartilages embedded in the aryepiglottic folds. #Structure #Cartilage #CartilagesAndBones #GS7
    Function→Support and stiffen the aryepiglottic folds. #Function #Cartilage #CartilagesAndBones #GS7
    
7
    Identification→Tracheal cartilage #Identification #Cartilage #AirwayFramework #GS7
    Structure→C-shaped hyaline cartilage rings open posteriorly. #Structure #Cartilage #AirwayFramework #GS7

    Relations→Posterior gap is bridged by trachealis muscle and fibroelastic tissue. #Relations #Cartilage #AirwayFramework #GS7

8
    Identification→Thyrohyoid membrane (median thyrohyoid ligament part) #Identification #Membrane #MembranesLigaments #GS7

9
    Identification→Thyrohyoid membrane (lateral) #Identification #Membrane #MembranesLigaments #GS7

    Contents→Pierced by the internal laryngeal nerve and superior laryngeal vessels. #Contents #Membrane #MembranesLigaments #GS7

10

Arteries and nerves

24
    Identification→Superior thyroid artery #Identification #Artery #ArteriesAndNerves #GS7
    Origin→Usually the first anterior branch of the external carotid artery. #Origin #Artery #ArteriesAndNerves #GS7
    Course→Descends toward the superior pole of the thyroid gland. #Course #Artery #ArteriesAndNerves #GS7
    Branches→Gives the superior laryngeal artery among other branches. #Branches #Artery #ArteriesAndNerves #GS7

25
    Identification→Superior laryngeal artery #Identification #Artery #ArteriesAndNerves #GS7
    Origin→Usually a branch of the superior thyroid artery. #Origin #Artery #ArteriesAndNerves #GS7
    Course→Pierces the thyrohyoid membrane with the internal laryngeal nerve. #Course #Artery #ArteriesAndNerves #GS7
    Supply→Supplies the upper laryngeal mucosa and structures. #Supply #Artery #ArteriesAndNerves #GS7
    
26
    Identification→Superior laryngeal nerve #Identification #Nerve #ArteriesAndNerves #GS7
    Roots→Branch of the vagus nerve (CN X). #Roots #Nerve #ArteriesAndNerves #GS7
    Course→Descends from the vagus and divides into internal and external branches near the hyoid/larynx. #Course #Nerve #ArteriesAndNerves #GS7
    Motor functions→External branch supplies cricothyroid and contributes to inferior pharyngeal constrictor innervation. #Motor #Nerve #ArteriesAndNerves #GS7
    Sensory functions→Internal branch provides sensation above the vocal folds and forms an important afferent limb of the cough reflex. #Sensory #Nerve #ArteriesAndNerves #GS7
    Lesion→External branch injury causes reduced ability to produce high-pitched sounds and voice fatigue; internal branch injury reduces supraglottic sensation and the cough reflex, increasing aspiration risk. #Lesion #Nerve #ArteriesAndNerves #GS7
    
27
    Identification→Recurrent laryngeal nerve #Identification #Nerve #ArteriesAndNerves #GS7
    Roots→Branch of the vagus nerve (CN X). #Roots #Nerve #ArteriesAndNerves #GS7
    Course→Right loops under the subclavian artery; left loops under the aortic arch near the ligamentum arteriosum; both ascend in the tracheo-oesophageal groove and enter the larynx as inferior laryngeal nerves. #Course #Nerve #ArteriesAndNerves #GS7
    Motor functions→Supplies all intrinsic laryngeal muscles except cricothyroid. #Motor #Nerve #ArteriesAndNerves #GS7
    Sensory functions→Supplies laryngeal mucosa below the vocal folds. #Sensory #Nerve #ArteriesAndNerves #GS7
    Lesion→Unilateral injury causes hoarseness from ipsilateral vocal-fold paralysis; bilateral injury can cause severe airway obstruction because both vocal folds may remain near the midline. #Lesion #Nerve #ArteriesAndNerves #GS7
    Clinical relevance→At risk in thyroid surgery and mediastinal pathology; the left nerve may be affected by aortic arch enlargement. #Clinical #Nerve #ArteriesAndNerves #GS7
