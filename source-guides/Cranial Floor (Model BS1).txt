    Identification→Optic nerve (CN II) #Identification #Nerve #BS1

26
    Identification→Oculomotor nerve (CN III) #Identification #Nerve #BS1
    Roots→Midbrain nuclei at the level of the superior colliculus; somatic motor and parasympathetic fibres. #Roots #Nerve #BS1
    Course→Emerges from ventral midbrain, passes between posterior cerebral and superior cerebellar arteries, travels in cavernous sinus wall and enters orbit via superior orbital fissure. #Course #Nerve #BS1

28
    Identification→Trigeminal nerve (CN V) #Identification #Nerve #BS1
    Roots→Large sensory root and smaller motor root emerging from lateral pons. #Roots #Nerve #BS1
    Course→Sensory root expands into trigeminal ganglion; divisions V1, V2 and V3 leave via superior orbital fissure, foramen rotundum and foramen ovale respectively. #Course #Nerve #BS1
    Motor functions→Motor fibres travel with V3 to muscles of mastication and selected first-arch muscles. #Motor #Nerve #BS1
    Sensory functions→Major somatic sensory nerve of face, cornea, nasal/oral cavities, teeth and much of cranial dura. #Sensory #Nerve #BS1
    Lesion→May cause ipsilateral facial sensory loss; motor root involvement causes weakness of mastication with the jaw deviating toward the side of the lesion on opening; the corneal reflex afferent limb may be impaired if V1 is involved.    #Lesion #Nerve #BS1

    Course→Passes in the lateral wall of the cavernous sinus and exits via foramen rotundum into the pterygopalatine fossa. #Course #Nerve #BS1

    Identification→Mandibular branch of trigeminal nerve (CN V3) #Identification #Nerve #BS1
    Course→Exits cranial cavity through foramen ovale. #Course #Nerve #BS1

33

    Roots→Abducens nucleus in caudal pons. #Roots #Nerve #BS1

35
    Identification→Facial nerve (CN VII) #Identification #Nerve #BS1
    Roots→Motor nucleus of facial nerve in pons, with sensory/parasympathetic fibres travelling in nervus intermedius. #Roots #Nerve #BS1
    Course→Enters internal acoustic meatus, traverses facial canal, exits stylomastoid foramen and branches within parotid gland. #Course #Nerve #BS1

37
    Identification→Glossopharyngeal nerve (CN IX) #Identification #Nerve #BS1

38
    Identification→Vagus nerve (CN X) #Identification #Nerve #BS1
