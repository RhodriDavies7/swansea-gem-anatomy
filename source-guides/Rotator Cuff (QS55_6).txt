    Identification→Sternocleidomastoid muscle #Identification #Muscle #AttachmentSite #QS55_6

    Identification→Pectoralis major muscle #Identification #Muscle #AttachmentSite #QS55_6
    Attachment on model→Lateral lip of the intertubercular sulcus of humerus; clavicular head also originates from medial half of clavicle. #Attachments #Muscle #AttachmentSite #QS55_6
    Actions→Adducts and medially rotates arm; clavicular head flexes arm; sternocostal head can extend a flexed arm. #Action #Muscle #AttachmentSite #QS55_6

3
    Identification→Deltoid muscle #Identification #Muscle #AttachmentSite #QS55_6
    Attachment on model→Origin from lateral third of clavicle, acromion and spine of scapula; inserts on deltoid tuberosity of humerus. #Attachments #Muscle #AttachmentSite #QS55_6
    Actions→Major abductor of arm from about 15–90°; anterior fibres flex/medially rotate, posterior fibres extend/laterally rotate. #Action #Muscle #AttachmentSite #QS55_6
    Innervation→Axillary nerve (C5–C6). #Innervation #Muscle #AttachmentSite #QS55_6
