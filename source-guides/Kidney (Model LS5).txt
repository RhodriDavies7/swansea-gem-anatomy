Renal regions
A
    Identification→Renal medulla #Identification #RenalRegion #RenalRegions #LS5
    Location→Inner part of the kidney deep to the renal cortex. #Location #RenalRegion #RenalRegions #LS5
    Structure→Composed mainly of renal pyramids containing loops of Henle, collecting ducts and vasa recta. #Structure #RenalRegion #RenalRegions #LS5
    Function→Establishes and maintains the medullary osmotic gradient required to concentrate urine. #Function #RenalRegion #RenalRegions #LS5
    Blood supply→Supplied by vasa recta arising from efferent arterioles of juxtamedullary nephrons. #BloodSupply #RenalRegion #RenalRegions #LS5
    
B
    Identification→Renal cortex #Identification #RenalRegion #RenalRegions #LS5
    Location→Outer renal tissue beneath the capsule, extending between pyramids as renal columns. #Location #RenalRegion #RenalRegions #LS5
    Structure→Contains renal corpuscles and proximal/distal convoluted tubules. #Structure #RenalRegion #RenalRegions #LS5
    Function→Major site of glomerular filtration and tubular reabsorption/secretion. #Function #RenalRegion #RenalRegions #LS5
    Blood supply→Receives blood through cortical radiate (interlobular) arteries and afferent arterioles. #BloodSupply #RenalRegion #RenalRegions #LS5
    
Renal vessels
1
    Identification→Renal vein #Identification #Vein #RenalVessels #LS5
    Formation→Formed by convergence of intrarenal venous channels at the renal hilum. #Formation #Vein #RenalVessels #LS5
    Course→Leaves the kidney anterior to the renal artery and drains into the inferior vena cava. #Course #Vein #RenalVessels #LS5
    Relations→At the hilum the usual anterior-to-posterior arrangement is renal vein, renal artery, renal pelvis/ureter. #Relations #Vein #RenalVessels #LS5
    Clinical relevance→The left renal vein passes anterior to the aorta and beneath the superior mesenteric artery and may be compressed in nutcracker syndrome. #Clinical #Vein #RenalVessels #LS5
    
2
    Identification→Renal artery #Identification #Artery #RenalVessels #LS5
    Origin→Direct lateral branch of the abdominal aorta, usually around L1–L2. #Origin #Artery #RenalVessels #LS5
    Course→Runs toward the renal hilum; the right renal artery passes posterior to the inferior vena cava. #Course #Artery #RenalVessels #LS5
    Branches→Divides into segmental arteries, then interlobar, arcuate and interlobular arteries. #Branches #Artery #RenalVessels #LS5
    Supply→Supplies renal parenchyma and gives rise ultimately to afferent arterioles of glomeruli. #Supply #Artery #RenalVessels #LS5
    Clinical relevance→Segmental arteries are effectively end arteries, so occlusion may cause segmental renal infarction. #Clinical #Artery #RenalVessels #LS5

Collecting system and renal parenchyma
3
    Identification→Ureter #Identification #Tube #CollectingSystem #LS5
    Function→Transports urine from renal pelvis to urinary bladder by peristalsis. #Function #Tube #CollectingSystem #LS5
    Course→Descends retroperitoneally from renal pelvis, crosses pelvic brim and enters bladder obliquely. #Course #Tube #CollectingSystem #LS5
    Constrictions→Classically at pelviureteric junction, crossing of iliac vessels/pelvic brim, and vesicoureteric junction. #Constrictions #Tube #CollectingSystem #LS5
    Clinical relevance→These constrictions are common sites for ureteric stone impaction. #Clinical #Tube #CollectingSystem #LS5
    
4
    Identification→Pelvis of the kidney #Identification #CollectingStructure #CollectingSystem #LS5
    Structure→Funnel-shaped expanded proximal part of the ureter within the renal sinus. #Structure #CollectingStructure #CollectingSystem #LS5
    Formation→Formed by union of major calyces. #Formation #CollectingStructure #CollectingSystem #LS5
    Function→Collects urine from major calyces and funnels it into the ureter. #Function #CollectingStructure #CollectingSystem #LS5
    Relations→At the renal hilum it lies posterior to the renal artery and vein. #Relations #CollectingStructure #CollectingSystem #LS5
    
5
    Identification→Minor calyx #Identification #CollectingStructure #CollectingSystem #LS5
    Structure→Cup-shaped collecting space surrounding the apex of a renal papilla. #Structure #CollectingStructure #CollectingSystem #LS5
    Function→Receives urine from papillary ducts at the renal papilla. #Function #CollectingStructure #CollectingSystem #LS5
    Connections→Several minor calyces unite to form a major calyx. #Connections #CollectingStructure #CollectingSystem #LS5
    
6
    Identification→Renal papilla and papillary ducts #Identification #RenalStructure #CollectingSystem #LS5
    Location→Renal papilla is the apex of a renal pyramid projecting into a minor calyx. #Location #RenalStructure #CollectingSystem #LS5
    Structure→Papillary ducts are terminal collecting ducts that open at the area cribrosa of the papilla. #Structure #RenalStructure #CollectingSystem #LS5
    Function→Deliver urine from collecting ducts into the minor calyx. #Function #RenalStructure #CollectingSystem #LS5
    Also called→Papillary ducts are also called ducts of Bellini. #AlsoCalled #RenalStructure #CollectingSystem #LS5
    
7
    Identification→Renal pyramid #Identification #RenalStructure #CollectingSystem #LS5
    Location→Conical unit of renal medulla with base toward cortex and apex forming a renal papilla. #Location #RenalStructure #CollectingSystem #LS5
    Contents→Contains straight tubules, loops of Henle and collecting ducts arranged toward the papilla. #Contents #RenalStructure #CollectingSystem #LS5
    Function→Contributes to urine concentration and directs collecting ducts toward the papilla. #Function #RenalStructure #CollectingSystem #LS5
    Relations→Adjacent pyramids are separated by cortical extensions called renal columns. #Relations #RenalStructure #CollectingSystem #LS5
